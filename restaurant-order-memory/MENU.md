# Vic on George menu, as loaded into the drill

Vic on George, Level 3, Myer Sydney City, 436 George St, Sydney NSW 2000.
Fully licensed cafe-bar. Hours per the venue site: Mon–Fri 9:30–5:30,
Sat 9:30–6, Sun 10–6.

## How this was checked, and how it was not

Reconstructed on 15 Sep 2026 from search-engine listings of the venue site
(vicongeorge.com.au) and its Uber Eats store. The build environment's
network policy returned 403 at the proxy for the venue site, Uber Eats,
Menuspot, Restaurant Guru, Tripadvisor, the two 2018 blog write-ups and the
Wayback Machine, so no page was opened or screenshotted. Every item below is
therefore a listing excerpt, not a photo of the printed menu. Treat it as a
draft to correct against the board on shift.

Source keys: **Site** = vicongeorge.com.au listing; **UE** = Uber Eats
listing; **Reviews** = Tripadvisor / Restaurant Guru reviews; **2018** =
two April 2018 blog posts.

were not verified for this venue.
