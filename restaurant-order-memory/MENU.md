# Vic on George menu, as loaded into the drill

Vic on George, Level 3, Myer Sydney City, 450 George St, Sydney NSW 2000
(Uber Eats lists 450; the venue site lists 436, which is Myer's main
address). Fully licensed cafe-bar. Venue site hours: Mon–Fri 9:30–5:30,
Sat 9:30–6, Sun 10–6. Uber Eats menu hours: 10:00 am – 3:00 pm.

## How this was checked

The Uber Eats store page text was supplied directly on 15 Sep 2026, so the
food, snacks, sauces, cold drinks and sweets below are verbatim from it,
with prices. Uber Eats is the delivery menu, not the whole in-venue menu:
it carries no coffee or tea. Those, and four dishes that appear only in the
venue site's search listing, are in the second section and remain
listing excerpts, because the site itself could not be opened from the
build environment (egress policy, 403 at the proxy).

## Prices shown on the drill's tiles

Food, snacks, sauces and sweets carry the Uber Eats prices above. Combo
meal prices include chips or salad and a drink, so a rung ticket's total
will run higher than the venue's actual bill when a drink is added on top.
Drinks carry the venue site's in-venue prices (soft drinks $7.50, juice
$8.50, Pellegrino $7.50, still $6.50, coffees from $4.60), which differ
from Uber Eats. Large coffee is +$1.00 per the site. Items with no listed
price show none. Edit any line in the menu panel with a `$price` suffix.

## Mods in the drill

Size (large), milk (oat, almond, soy, skim), extra shot, decaf, extra hot,
eggs poached / scrambled / fried, the site's eggs-your-way extras as
"Add …", the six Uber Eats sauces as "Add …", and generic removals (no
bacon, no cheese, no onion, no pickles, no egg, sauce on the side, no ice).
Milk options are standard cafe assumptions and were not verified.

## Corrections made once the real listing was read

The earlier reconstruction from search snippets had: "prawn roll" (it is
the Prawn Poboy, and the second description belonged to the Prawn Panko
snack); "falafel wrap" (it is a Falafel Salad served with a tortilla);
"club sandwich" (Victoria Club); "pulled pork ciabatta" (Cuban Sandwich);
"salmon ceviche" (Salmon Ceviche Nachos); no Prawn Toast (real, $26); no
Seafood Croquettes; no sweets at all (ten items, including their most-liked
dish); and no sauces. All fixed.

## Left out

Pasta, risotto, haloumi salads and the 2018 cakes appear only in old
reviews and blogs and on no current listing.
