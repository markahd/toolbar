# Continuity sheet

The canon rules are in BRIEF.md section 2 and win over anything here.

## Canon facts (short version)

- Atsuya Kusakabe. Grade 1, Tokyo Jujutsu High. No innate technique. Katana. New Shadow Style; Simple Domain (call it "the circle" most of the time). The reach of one arm and one sword.
- Younger sister, **never named**. Memory loss in patches, paralysis, wheelchair, window chair. Her son Takeru died young in an accident. Yaga gave Takeru's soul an independent cursed-corpse form. Kusakabe keeps this secret completely. Never show Takeru's corpse after Yaga dies. Ch 21 onward does not mention it at all.
- The debt to Yaga feels like a coin in his pocket that he checks.
- 2017 (Ch 1–6): Maki, Panda, Inumaki and Yuta are FIRST-years. Kusakabe is NOT their homeroom teacher yet. He knows them by sight. No training-yard scenes with them as his class before Ch 7.
- 2018 (Ch 7+): homeroom teacher of the second-years Maki, Panda, Inumaki. Yuta is abroad.
- Yuji Itadori is **fifteen** in 2018 (canon). Use "fifteen" if an age is ever stated, including the Ch 24 thought ("earnest, idiotic, fifteen").
- Night Parade: 24 Dec 2017, Shinjuku (and Kyoto). Heroes of the night: Yuta and Gojo.
- Shibuya: 31 Oct 2018. Order of the night in this book: Ch 13 curtain and upper levels with Panda -> Ch 14 B5F lie -> Ch 15 Sukuna vs Jogo sensed, Sukuna's "don't move" -> Ch 16 the collision, under the rock, Panda lifts it -> Ch 17 the lull: news in fragments over radio and from auxiliaries (Nanami dead, Inumaki badly hurt and his left arm lost, Maki badly burned, Ijichi stabbed but alive), stretchers -> Ch 18 Kenjaku in Geto's body, Maximum: Uzumaki, Kusakabe deflects it to protect Miwa, Kenjaku impressed that someone of this generation still uses old-style real sorcery; Kenjaku vs Choso; Uraume freezes Kusakabe (with Panda and Miwa nearby); Yuki Tsukumo arrives and breaks him out; dawn.
- After: Yaga arrested and sentenced to death; Panda held to lure him; Kusakabe frees Panda offstage (never shown, never discussed). Yaga executed by Gakuganji (offstage). Kusakabe supports executing Yuji quickly; Yuta assigned as executioner (offstage, Kusakabe only hears of it). Culling Game begins; Kusakabe is not a player, stays in Tokyo. Gojo released from the Prison Realm in December. Shinjuku set for 24 Dec 2018. Kusakabe trains Yuji in the month before.
- Maki after Shibuya: burn scars. Inumaki: no left arm, still speaks little.
- Epilogue: two long scars across his chest; head of the New Shadow Style, old binding vows lifted; broken sword in a cupboard.

## Side characters (fan-invented, keep names fixed)

- **Mrs. Fujita** — sister's carer, fifties, brisk, decent, overworked. Raises rates (Ch 2). Quits (Ch 5).
- **Ono-san (Ono Haruka)** — new carer from Ch 5 on. Late twenties, practical, few words, not warm, competent. Stays.
- **Mr. Hasegawa** — coordinator at Midori Home Care agency (Ch 5).
- **Okabe** — mid-level administrative official at the school/headquarters. Pedantic about reports (Ch 1, can reappear briefly).
- **Kamo Tsubasa** — nineteen, Grade 2, minor branch of the Kamo clan. Contemptuous of technique-less sorcerers. Takes credit (Ch 4).
- **Nurse Mori** — night nurse, children's ward, curt and exhausted (Ch 3).
- **Inspector Sakaguchi** — official who interrogates the Inumaki incident and files a complaint (Ch 8).
- **Zen'in Masaru** — visiting Zen'in branch-house representative (Ch 9). Not a canon Zen'in.
- **Kurosawa** — recruiter for Hoshino Security, private security for the wealthy Takamine family (Ch 11).
- Sister's flat: twenty minutes by train from the school. His flat: walking distance to the school.
