# Chapter 18. Uzumaki: summary

Shibuya, 1 November 2018, after two until dawn. Panda finds Kusakabe at the side-street vending machine, the second can in his pocket: Yuji and Todo are fighting the patchwork curse, "anyone who can." "Fine." They meet Kasumi Miwa, a frightened Kyoto second-year who holds her sword the New Shadow way. At the ruined open ground they find Yuji kneeling, Todo missing a hand, and a dead man: Suguru Geto's face, voice and robes, black stitches across the forehead. He folds the beaten curse into a ball and swallows it, then fires Maximum: Uzumaki at Yuji. Miwa steps in and freezes. In a tenth of a second Kusakabe is there, and turns the spiral aside like a river round a stone. Kenjaku is impressed by real, old sorcery. "Why didn't I avoid that?" Choso attacks; Uraume freezes Kusakabe, Panda and Miwa. Yuki Tsukumo breaks him out. Dawn, thawing, his hands.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Time: Panda finds him "after two" at the side-street vending machine (end of Ch 17). The second can went in his LEFT coat pocket unopened; it froze in the ice, split, and he left it standing upright on a slab at dawn.
- Miwa: blue shoulder-length hair, Kyoto uniform, black scabbard; holds it New Shadow style (left hand under the guard, thumb ready). Calls him "Kusakabe-san", Panda "Panda-kun", Yuji "Itadori-kun". - Kenjaku as seen: Geto's long black hair tied up, heavy earrings, dull gold and black monk's robes, black stitches across the forehead "like a seam on a coat"; folds the beaten curse into a plum-sized ball and swallows it. - Kusakabe knows Geto from a wanted photograph pinned in the dispatch office for a year (corners curled, colour gone orange).
- Yuji seen only from behind, kneeling, then later sitting with his head in his hands. Kusakabe STILL has not seen Yuji's face up close. Todo seen sitting against rubble, one hand gone (wrapped in red cloth), grinning. Neither named on the page except "Todo" by Miwa/Panda.
- Choso (not named; Kusakabe doesn't know who he is): pale, thin, hair in two short bunches, dark band across the bridge of his nose, shouting about "brothers"; blood drawn out in lines like wire. Kusakabe compares it to Kamo Tsubasa (Ch 4).
- Uraume (not named): white hair cut short, pale face, pale quiet clothes; never crossed his circle, "simply there"; said something quiet to the man in robes. Kusakabe can't tell age or sex and stops trying. The ice froze Kusakabe, Panda (mid-word) and Miwa (her hand gripping the back of his coat).
