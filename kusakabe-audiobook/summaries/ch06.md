# Chapter 6. The Night of a Hundred Demons: summary

December 2017. Kusakabe is on a job in Kawagoe when Suguru Geto walks into Jujutsu High and declares the Night Parade: a thousand curses each in Shinjuku and Kyoto at sundown on the twenty-fourth, a Sunday. He hears it four times, secondhand. The dispatch board lists "Shinjuku. All available." He pays Ono double to cover Sunday overnight, and visits his sister early that morning; she knows him. At sundown in Shinjuku, under Christmas illuminations, curses pour from the alleys. His body saves a fleeing family before the thought finishes; they never look back and leave a cake box. Then the methodical night: curse, blade, next, through a looping Christmas playlist, a cut forearm, radio fragments, a crying spear-user, a drunk salaryman paying him in a mandarin. After midnight the curses lose direction. At dawn, on a kerb with vending-machine coffee, he hears from Tanabe that Okkotsu and Gojo are the heroes of the night. He crushes the can, goes home and sleeps fourteen hours.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Kusakabe was on a job in Kawagoe (a primary school storeroom, a curse in folded flags) the day Geto came to the school; he heard the declaration from four people: a first-year auxiliary at the gate, a loud Grade 2, the dispatch coordinator, Okabe. Ijichi drove him back.
- He remembers Geto only as a name on lists, then "a village, and a number nobody liked to say".
- Dispatch board for 24 Dec, one red line: "Shinjuku. All available. Kyoto: see Kyoto."
- Ono Haruka covered Christmas Eve afternoon and overnight at double rate ("Agency rule. Holidays"); asked the night carer to stay till she came. Her texts: 18:40 "She asked where you were. Told her work. She said 'he's always working.'" and 06:12 "Overnight done. She slept. I've signed the sheet for double. Merry Christmas." He replied "Thanks."
- Sunday 24 Dec morning visit: sister knew him ("You're early"), drank thickened tea, laughed at a man forcing a Christmas tree into a tiny car through the passenger window. "You're always working."
- Shinjuku posting: side streets east of the station (pachinko parlour, ramen shop with a paper lantern, a closed bar). Briefed by a young unnamed auxiliary with a fogging laminated map ("Who comes?" / "Someone."). Gojo "somewhere west of the station", then gone (radio).
- Injury: a shallow cut on his forearm around seven; coat sleeve stiff and brown; coat bagged "to deal with another year". Left shoe heel finally gave out.
- Unnamed Grade 2 woman, short hair, long spear, cried steadily while fighting well.
- Tanabe (or Tanaka; Kusakabe isn't sure): Grade 2, thirties, broad, split lip, jacket missing a sleeve; told him about the school: Geto went for Okkotsu and the girl-curse; Maki, Inumaki and Panda hurt badly but alive; Okkotsu (sixteen) beat Geto, Gojo finished it. "Long one." / "Yeah."
- Kusakabe has seen Okkotsu in corridors ("a way of apologising with his shoulders", something behind him "the air didn't like") and kept his distance.
- He slept fourteen hours from Christmas morning to Christmas night, in his clothes, on top of the covers.
