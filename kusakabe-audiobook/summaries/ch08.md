# Chapter 8. Rice Ball Words: summary

A Thursday in mid-May 2018. Dispatch pairs Kusakabe (supervising) with Inumaki for second-year field hours: a karaoke building in Kita ward, closed six weeks, with singing from empty rooms. In Ijichi's car they sit apart in silence; Kusakabe thinks they look alike from outside but aren't: one is careful, one is tired. Inumaki drinks throat syrup and takes a lozenge. Kusakabe orders him not to use Cursed Speech without permission. On the fourth floor, a curse of many singing mouths drops from the ceiling and goes for the new manager, Kanda, who came in with his own key. Too far to reach, Kusakabe runs; Inumaki says "Don't move" twice, freezing curse and man. Kusakabe cuts it. Kanda falls and sprains his wrist; Inumaki coughs blood. At the clinic, Inspector Sakaguchi demands a statement from the boy. Kusakabe gives it instead, briefly. Sakaguchi files a complaint on his supervision and notes the boy's file. Inumaki shows "thanks" on his phone. On a bench: lozenge, bad coffee, silence, then a hoarse "Salmon."

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Mission: mid-May 2018, a Thursday, car at one. Karaoke building by the station in Kita ward: five storeys of orange tile between a phone-case shop and a dentist; roof sign "SING" with the G dead; banner "CLOSED FOR RENOVATION"; tiny cordon of yellow tape from lamp post to drainpipe. Young auxiliary with a clipboard and a very new suit. Curse was stronger than the file's "Grade 2, probably": a pour of singing mouths from the fourth-floor ceiling. Two cuts.
- Dispatch sheet: "Kusakabe A. (Grade 1, supervising). Inumaki T. (Semi-Grade 1, second year). Field experience." Second-years need supervised field hours (principal's list). Dispatch coordinator ate a rice cracker.
- Inumaki's kit: a small brown bottle of throat syrup (smells of cherries and paint) and a flat tin of honey-coloured wrapped lozenges. He folds wrappers into tiny squares and pockets them. He coughed blood (palm, inside of collar) after saying "Don't move" twice; the second time his voice tore. Afterwards a white dressing under his jaw; his voice "like gravel in a bucket". Glosses used: Kelp (hello), Tuna mayo (depends, also as a hand tilt), Salmon (yes/fine). He writes on his phone.
- Kusakabe's rule to him: "Stay in my reach. Behind me, left. Don't use it unless I tell you." Inumaki broke it to save the civilian.
- Mr. Kanda: new manager, cardigan, tape measure, came in the back at twelve with his own key, told the building was being fumigated. Couldn't see the curse. Sprained wrist. Thinks a teenager drugged him ("Is this a cult?"), talking about a lawyer.
- The school's clinic: small grey three-floor building in a residential street, tiny car park, no meaningful sign; old throat doctor with very clean hands who asks nothing; waiting-room tank with one old orange fish; side room with a hand-washing poster; vending machine by the door; bench under a small tree.
- Inspector Sakaguchi: late thirties, navy suit, no tie, rimless glasses, slim leather folder, small black recorder; headquarters, Office of Field Conduct. Arrived 5:10 p.m. Small neat handwriting that doesn't slope. Same depth of nod for everyone. Filed a formal complaint on Kusakabe's supervision (on his record, next to "Froze?") and a note on Inumaki's file for "unauthorised use". Not a villain: he can only verify a civilian hurt by a restricted technique. "I'll write that you said it."
- Kusakabe's statement (his longest speech so far) ends: "Two words. The man has a sprained wrist. Without those two words, he doesn't have a wrist. Or an arm to put it on."
- Kusakabe has the boy's blood on his cuff and leaves it.
