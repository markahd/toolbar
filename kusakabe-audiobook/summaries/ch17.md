# Chapter 17. The Count: summary

Shibuya, 1 November 2018, midnight to after two. Kusakabe and Panda walk out of the ruin to a field point at the top of Meiji-dori. A young auxiliary writes names on a van's side in two unheaded columns: living and dead. The radio says Ijichi was stabbed but lives. A head-lamped medic puts Kusakabe on stretchers with an auxiliary, Mizuno; Panda goes to dig. Looking south, Kusakabe sees the ruin's edge is a clean circle. He counts fifty-two steps per trip. Passing voices say Maki was badly burned. Around one, the radio confirms Nanami's death; the auxiliary writes NANAMI K. in the right column. "See you" replays; he keeps carrying. Panda brings word that Inumaki lost his left arm. Kusakabe sits with it, silent; Panda sits apart, then goes back out. Sent down a dark side street, Kusakabe finds a lit vending machine, buys two coffees and holds the second until it cools.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Field point at the top of Meiji-dori (where Ch 15's civilian van was): three vans, two cars with headlights angled in. Names in marker on the white side panel of the nearest van, two columns with no headings (left: alive/accounted for, long; right: the dead). KUSAKABE and PANDA are on the left. NANAMI K. written in the right column around 1 a.m.
- Young male auxiliary with the pen: black suit, grey-white shirt, cut along his jaw closed with three tape strips; Ijichi drove him in that afternoon and "said sorry about the traffic." Kusakabe gave him the second half of his water.
- Woman medic with a head-lamp: fifties, short grey hair, blue gloves bloody to the wrist, curt ("Can you lift?" / "Then lift." / "Again."). Sent him to check the side street: "Don't be a hero." / "Wasn't planning to." / "Good. We've got enough of those in the right-hand column."
- **Mizuno**: auxiliary wearing a fire brigade jacket taken from an empty fire engine's back seat; carried stretchers with Kusakabe ("Lie down, sir").
- Fifty-two steps from the rubble edge to the vans (fifty-four round a car). They ran out of stretchers and used an office door.
- Kudo: boy of about twelve in a skeleton costume looking for his mother ("She was wearing a crown"); left with an auxiliary who keeps a list of children.
- News as received: Ijichi stabbed, out, alive (radio). Maki burned, bad, by "the fire one"; was with the Zen'in men, "the old one"; taken out in the first car, talking and swearing (passing voices). Nanami confirmed dead on the radio; "Someone saw. It was a curse. The one that does things to people. The shapes of people." (auxiliary). Inumaki: Panda heard from a woman auxiliary who helped get him out near the station; alive, taken somewhere to be worked on, left arm gone in the collision.
- Tanabe (or Tanaka) at the field point, eye dressed: "You held your stairs." / "I didn't go back down. In the end." / "Nobody could have... That's what they're saying." Kusakabe has still not confessed the lie.
- Kusakabe still has Ch 10's waxed paper square in one coat pocket. He used the cold hundred-yen coin for the SECOND coffee. It's gone now.
- With Nanami dead, nobody at school knows about his sister again.
- Panda is out digging (last sent to Dogenzaka) when the chapter ends; Kusakabe is alone in the side street, east off Meiji-dori, a block down, around 2 a.m.
