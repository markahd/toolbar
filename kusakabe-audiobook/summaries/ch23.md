# Chapter 23. Training Yuji: summary

Monday 17 to Saturday 22 December 2018. A handwritten sheet assigns Kusakabe to train Itadori Yuji, afternoons "two till dark", until further notice. He doesn't look for a way out. In the yard he sees Yuji's face up close for the first time: ordinary, fifteen, brown eyes, small healed lines under them. His circle finds only a boy. Asked if there was traffic, he says "No." Drill: a heel line at his reach; Yuji must touch his shoulder without being tapped. Yuji keeps glancing at his feet. On Tuesday: "Stop thinking about where your feet go. Feel the edge. Let the body answer." It works. Wednesday Yuji apologises for Shibuya; "Wasn't you. I saw." Reading drills, then soybeans with eyes shut (the edge, not the circle, which old vows forbid him to teach). Yuji thanks him constantly; Kusakabe keeps the unsaid confession. Panda watches from the edges all week, and on Saturday at dusk sits beside him on the third step while Yuji trains alone.
