# Chapter 16. Under the Rock: summary

Late on 31 October 2018 in Shibuya, Kusakabe prises his fingers off his hilt, then senses Sukuna meet something huge and turning to the south. His circle fills with endless cuts from every side, then fire, then a pressure that brings the building down. He wakes on his back in the dark, in a pocket under an angled slab, his drawn katana in a grip he doesn't remember. He doesn't move, call out, or use his phone light. He reckons: his sister and who would tell her (only Nanami knew), Takeru on her lap, the unpaid debt to Yaga, the stain, the sofa, vanished days, his avoidances, B5F. Not afraid, lost. He stays silent when rescuers pass. Panda lifts the slab: "Sensei. You alive?" He takes the paw for the first time. "Let's go." "Okay, sensei." They walk through grey ruins, Kusakabe half a step behind.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- He doesn't know where Panda was when it came down; Panda found him by searching ("Kept lifting things"), leaving a long line of turned-over rubble behind him.
- Sukuna vs Mahoraga experienced only as two presences meeting to the south: one he knows, one "huge", "turning", like a mill wheel that never stops. Malevolent Shrine experienced as the circle filled with cuts from every side; then a line of fire from the south; then a flattening pressure. He never learns names.
- Injuries: forearm cut reopened; left side aching; nothing serious. Panda's black cape-cloth bandage on his left knuckles is still on; he takes Panda's paw with that hand. First time he accepts the paw (refused twice in Ch 13). Not spelled out.
- Panda: fur grey with dust and dark with blood, one ear torn at the edge.
- Kusakabe heard voices of searchers pass over the rubble and deliberately stayed silent (didn't want to be dug out and questioned).
- Facts used: 31 Oct 2018 is a Wednesday; physio comes Thursdays (already paid); Sunday is four days off. Nobody at school knows about the sister except Nanami (Kusakabe doesn't yet know Nanami is dead).
- He keeps two keys that matter: his flat and his sister's (where he rings first anyway).
- The katana is still drawn and in his hand at the end; he hasn't sheathed it.
- He has NOT said sorry or thank you to Panda.
