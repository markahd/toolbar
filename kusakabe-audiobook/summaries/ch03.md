# Chapter 3. The Hurricane with Sunglasses: summary

Early November 2017, a few days after Chapter 2. Kusakabe is hiding on a bench outside the old archive room, reading a three-year-old paper-towel supplier list, when Gojo (blindfolded, eating powdered-sugar pastries) ambushes him. A curse in a children's ward is feeding on fear; Gojo is off to Kyoto. Kusakabe says "I'm busy," "Send another Grade 1," and "I don't want to," then hears himself ask what time it starts. Ijichi drives him to the hospital at ten. Night nurse Mori is curt and tells him room four's night light is broken ("It's been Monday for two weeks"). At 2:52 the corridor lights die; a curse built from children's night terrors pours from the playroom toward room four. Two cuts, done. Unseen, he fixes the rabbit night light with surgical tape. Next day Gojo, in sunglasses, calls him "best Grade 1 without a technique" in the staff room. Monday the dispatch board lists him four times. On the residual check, room four is empty; the rabbit still glows.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- His hiding place: bench outside the old archive room, radiator warm at one end. By the end of the chapter it has a box of files and someone's coat on it.
- Gojo calls him "Ku-sa-ka-be." Gojo knows he hides; Gojo went through "everybody's desk"; Kusakabe's is the tidiest and has a dying plant on it (staff room).
- Some staff privately call Gojo "a hurricane": you don't argue, you go indoors and find out afterwards where your things ended up.
- Gojo's dislike of one other unnamed Grade 1 ("he'd go in loud"); another Grade 1 in Hokkaido with a broken collarbone.
- Hospital: big grey general hospital in the east of the city; paediatric ward on the fifth floor; eleven children aged two to nine; painted animals (a row of ducks, a giraffe whose neck goes round the corner so its face is out of sight from the lift); doors open a hand's width; playroom at the far end with a smiling-sun sign; Kusakabe wore a CONTRACTOR lanyard.
- Nurse Mori: forties, blue scrubs, grey cardigan, hair clip "losing the argument", cold tea with a skin on it. Rules: don't wake them, knock quietly ("They're not animals in a zoo"), don't touch drip stands or trolleys. "Nobody reads what I write." / "Someone did." Never thanks him. Unnamed young agency nurse with a ponytail.
- Room four: a girl, Yui (name card with a strawberry sticker). Discharged before the residual check.
- The night light: a cheap plastic rabbit sitting up; he bent its prong with his door key and wrapped it in white surgical tape torn with his teeth. Still glowing in the empty room at the end.
- Report filed from the car, on time for once: "Exorcised at 02:54."
- Ijichi: "It's quite often not my fault. I find I'm still quite often sorry."
- Dispatch coordinator: woman in her thirties, headset always round her neck, small square handwriting. Dispatch board the next Monday: Chiba closed-down school (Wed), multistorey car park by the docks (Thu; Kusakabe grazed his hand on a pillar), a shrine in the hills (Sat, "Requested. Per G."), and the residual check (Thu night).
- Okabe present for the praise; took out a pen. "Reports by Friday. All of them."
- Gojo brought Kyoto sweets in green-and-gold paper; gave Kusakabe a pale green triangular one. He put it in his drawer uneaten.
