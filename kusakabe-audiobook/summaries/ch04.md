# Chapter 4. Paperwork: summary

November 2017. Since Gojo's loud praise, Kusakabe's files have thickened (three missions in nine days). A typed notice summons him to a mandatory panel review of Grade 1 deployment, "readiness, workload and conduct", at headquarters on Thursday. He gets as far as the corridor outside Conference Room B, sees the single empty chair facing the panel, and leaves by a fire door wedged open with a mop bucket. He spends the afternoon writing his overdue reports, on time for once. Next morning Okabe hands him a panel-assigned job: a Grade 1 curse in a disused railway tunnel in Gunma, with Kamo Tsubasa, nineteen, "accompanying for field experience". Tsubasa sneers at technique-less sorcerers, ignores Kusakabe's order to stay in his reach, overspends his blood and nearly dies. Kusakabe takes three steps and cuts the curse once. Tsubasa claims the kill, by phone and in the draft report. Someone upstairs pencils "Froze?" in the margin. Kusakabe signs.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- The higher-ups' "Review of Grade 1 Field Deployment, Tokyo Region": headquarters (grey stone building, plastic-looking potted plant), third floor, Conference Room B, Thursday 2 p.m. Kusakabe's name last on the list. He walked out before his turn. One panel member is bald and very still; another speaks like a man never interrupted. Kusakabe suspects the bald one wrote "Froze?".
- The panel then assigned him the Gunma mission directly (red-tabbed file).
- Recent missions (post-Gojo praise): a shrine in Chiba, a school gym in Kawasaki, a car park in Kawasaki that turned out to be two car parks.
- His staffroom desk is in the corner by a radiator that doesn't work (it ticks anyway).
- Gunma job: disused railway tunnel on a closed mountain line, brick arch, rusted gate; two road maintenance workers missing nine days (found dead, orange jackets); an earlier Grade 2 came out with a broken arm. Curse spread flat across the tunnel roof, dropped "like a sheet pulled off a bed"; smelled of iron/old coins. One cut.
- Kamo Tsubasa: nineteen, tall, narrow, pressed uniform, expensive haircut, leather strap of blood packs across his chest (Kamo blood manipulation; fires blood in lines). Calls him "Kusakabe" with no -san. Has an uncle ("Thank you, Uncle") who says technique-less sorcerers are "basically auxiliaries with swords." Signature slopes forward.
- Procedure: on joint missions the junior drafts the report, the senior countersigns. A separate-statement form exists (Ijichi offered to find it; Kusakabe declined: "It's fine.").
- The draft says Kusakabe "remained at the entrance" and "assisted in the final exorcism of the weakened target." A margin note in pencil: "Froze?" It's now on file.
- Kusakabe carries a sports drink from a vending machine to every job and almost never drinks it; gave it to Tsubasa.
- Okabe: grey cardigan this time. Said Kusakabe's reports came in "On time."
