# Chapter 25. Sunday (Epilogue): summary

A Sunday in April 2019. In the hall cupboard Kusakabe never opens stands his broken sword, two pieces in a cleaning cloth, put there in January; a new sword hangs on the wall. A three-week-old letter from the New Shadow Style dojo names him head of the style and lifts the old vows, so he can teach the circle to anyone. "Promotion. The one thing I spent my whole life avoiding." Okabe confirms it's honorary: unpaid. He now teaches the circle to a few students on Tuesdays and Thursdays, late for all of them. Dressing, he touches the two long scars across his chest. He finally emails Mr. Sato about the stain. On the train his hand checks his pocket: only a key and a receipt. Ono notes "Stiff." His sister knows him straight away, calls the weak tea "Better". "You look tired." "Yeah. Long few months." "Were you working?" A run of images. "Yeah. Bit of work." She turns to the window. Nobody looks up. It feels like enough.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Time: April 2019, a Sunday (the window opened for the first time that year).
- Broken sword: two pieces wrapped in an old cleaning cloth, upright behind the vacuum cleaner, a broken-spoke umbrella and a box from an old desk, in the narrow hall cupboard by his front door. Put there in January. Never opened since. A new sword hangs on the wall; he's "mostly" used to its weight.
- The letter: thick paper, brush script, red seal pressed hard enough to dent the kitchen table; from the dojo he was never invited to, men he's met twice "both times standing up". Head of the New Shadow Style; old vows lifted (the short version of the old promise: "Not to show it to anyone outside the style"; the long version took "a whole afternoon and a great deal of kneeling").
- Okabe (two binders): not a grade, "a position", "Honorary" (unpaid).
- He teaches the circle on Tuesdays and Thursdays to a few unnamed students (one an unnamed second-year who asked about the promise). Late for all of them.
- Scars: two long, across the chest, one above the other, not quite parallel; pale, pink at the edges in the cold; pull when he reaches up, lifts, bends, or laughs.
- Water stain: he finally emailed Mr. Sato on that Sunday morning ("Dear Mr. Sato. There is a water stain on the ceiling above the wardrobe. It has been there some time. Kusakabe."). "Three and a half years."
- The pocket where the debt-coin was: a key and a forgotten receipt.
- Ono: coat on, "Good today", sister asked for him at nine; notebook entry "Brother here 10:15. Stiff." Next shift Tuesday.
- Sister knew him straight away ("Atsuya"), asked for the window opened wider, said the tea was "Better... You're getting the hang of it." Window view: a shortcut path across the corner of the car park to the station (sign asking people not to use it).
- No survivors or deaths at Shinjuku named. Gojo, Panda, Yuji, Miwa, Maki, Inumaki not stated alive or dead.
