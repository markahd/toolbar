# Chapter 14. B5F: summary

31 October 2018, about 20:40 to 22:00, inside the Shibuya curtain. The radio orders north teams to hold the north entrances, so Kusakabe and Panda hold a covered staircase on Meiji-dori that leads down to the concourse and, five floors below, the Fukutoshin platform. They send civilians up the street and cut down a curse wearing stacked rubber Halloween masks. At ten to nine Tanabe (or Tanaka) comes up carrying a hurt auxiliary and tells Kusakabe alone: special grades, more than one, on B5F with Gojo; nobody has come back up. A radio fragment repeats it. Panda says, "We should go down." Kusakabe, with a cold sliding feeling in his stomach, says, "I heard they've already got people down there." Panda's long look; "Okay, sensei." Relief, then shame: "Nobody's going to know." He feels small. He turns the radio down ("Battery"), steps to cover Panda's back against a curse user's beasts, lets Panda bandage his knuckles, and answers "Didn't say" when Panda asks who is down there. The radio stops mentioning B5F. Ordered south, Panda walks ahead down the middle of the street; Kusakabe can't look at his back for long.
