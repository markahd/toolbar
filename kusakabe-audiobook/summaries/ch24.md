# Chapter 24. December Twenty-Third: summary

Sunday 23 December 2018 into the dawn of Monday the 24th. At nine a stranger reads out the Shinjuku order at school: Gojo meets the King of Curses alone; if he loses, the others go in one after another. Kusakabe's name is mid-list; Okkotsu and Yuji are on it. He goes to his sister's early. Ono has already written "Brother came"; "Wednesday?" "Wednesday." He replaces the lavender diffuser early, makes the weak tea, changes the clock batteries, brushes her hair, and promises the sea "when it's warm". She doesn't ask what the work is: "Wrap up, then." He stays until the night carer comes. He pays Monday's bills on Sunday night, and writes his sister's details in the old cat notebook, left open on the table. Sleepless on the sofa, he laughs at the stain. He thinks about running, for a long time. Then Panda, Miwa, Yuji, the others, the last of the debt. "And I'm the adult." He checks the sword. Dawn. *Shikata ga nai.* The circle. He walks out the door.
