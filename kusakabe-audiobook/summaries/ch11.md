# Chapter 11. The Offer: summary

September 2018. The September meeting at headquarters takes eleven minutes: reprimand active, May complaint on file, allowance review deferred to next August, "subject to conduct". On the steps, Kurosawa of Hoshino Security, a former Grade 2, offers him the Takamine family's resident-sorcerer post in Hayama: a little under twice his pay, a free flat, a car, medical cover for dependants, four days on and three off, no students or higher-ups. Kusakabe denies having family and nearly says yes. For days he keeps a two-column list on the back of a Midori envelope. Ono reports the doctor wants physio twice a week. Yaga passes in a corridor; the debt won't go on paper. On a Sunday his sister says he's doing sums and wants to go to the sea; he weighs Hayama's 1h52 journey against twenty minutes and lost Sundays. He refuses Kurosawa, books physio once a week, reopens it at 11pm, deletes the number and stares at the stain.

**New names and facts**

Reprimand active (twelve months from June); allowance review deferred to August 2019, "subject to conduct". Okabe said "Thank you, Kusakabe-san."

Four incidents in six years, nothing above Grade 3.
- The offer: a little under twice his salary, rent-free staff flat in Hayama, a car, comprehensive medical "for the employee and any dependants", four on/three off rota, Sundays "can't always be guaranteed". Open till end of September.
- Kusakabe told Kurosawa he has no family commitments ("No"). He told nobody at school about the offer. He refused by phone on the last Thursday of September (the 27th) from the archive-room bench; "May I ask why?" "No." Kurosawa: "Look after your knees."
- Hayama to the sister's flat: bus to Zushi, two changes, 1 hour 52 minutes.
- Physio: doctor recommends twice weekly; the agency has a physio woman Ono rates. Kusakabe booked ONCE a week, Thursdays, via Hasegawa ("For now"). He cut his phone plan to the cheapest. The sister liked a physio in hospital once.
- Kusakabe now answers Ono on the first ring (since December). Ono never says hello and always hangs up first.
- The sister wants to go to the sea "when it's warm"; "You could push me along the front." She forgot it within minutes. The upstairs dog has got worse.
- Kurosawa's number deleted from his phone at 11pm on 27 September. He never told Yaga.
