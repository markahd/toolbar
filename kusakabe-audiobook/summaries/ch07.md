# Chapter 7. Homeroom: summary

Late March to late April 2018. Yaga, sewing an ear onto a felt bear, makes Kusakabe homeroom teacher of the second-years from Monday 9 April; Gojo has the first-years, Okkotsu is abroad. Kusakabe arrives eleven minutes late. Maki asks why; "Traffic." She knows he walked and lets it go. He has no plan, so he sits on the third step and watches. On the Wednesday he tells Maki her back heel lifts before she strikes; it stays down after that. Okabe gives him a homeroom register; the attendance is already late. Panda translates Inumaki's rice-ball words. In a rainy classroom lesson Maki asks how he made Grade 1: "Practice." Panda, on the grass, notes he always sits facing the gate and asks if he ever thinks about quitting. Maki pushes him three days running to spar seriously; on the Friday he refuses in front of the others and she calls him lazy. "Probably." It stings. Monday, the "Traffic" arrangement survives. He watches them train and, for one breath, doesn't want to be elsewhere.

- Maki: glasses, ponytail, staff, short batons, a chain, bokken, something curved from the storehouse "not on the inventory." Never uses his name. The back-heel fix is in her feet now.

- Classroom: first floor of the old wooden building, end room, windows on wet trees, four desks; the fourth (Okkotsu's) pushed against the wall. Timetable: "Mon. 1st period: Theory (Homeroom)." Blue issue textbook on curse grading. Panda: "We did this last year." / "We did it again in February."

Kusakabe's whole reply: "Probably." His reasons, unspoken: the circle has no practice version; and she'd see there's no secret, just years.
