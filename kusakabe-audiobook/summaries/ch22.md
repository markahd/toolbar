# Chapter 22. Culling Season: summary

Mid-November to 21 December 2018. The dispatch board overflows onto taped printer paper; Kusakabe's name is on it seven times a week. A new driver, Sekine, takes him through a thinned, evacuating Tokyo. He learns of the Culling Game in fragments: Okabe's "Interim Guidance" sheet (ten colonies, two in Tokyo; non-players must not enter), noodle-shop TV, Ogawa. He hears the boy is dead, then that he isn't. He cuts a curse feeding on a hospital's evacuation. He visits his sister on weekdays; Ono asks where to take her if ordered to move: "Call me." "And if you don't answer?" "I'll answer." Panda passes him in a corridor with a bag, "Sensei," and goes; Panda and Maki are on the colony personnel list. On a compulsory rest day the sofa feels like the slab; he gets up, walks, and takes an unwanted night job: a curse lying on sleepers' chests in an evacuation gym. Gojo is released on the nineteenth. The board reads "24 Dec. Shinjuku." He circles the date on his kitchen calendar.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- **Sekine**: auxiliary driver, twenty-two, nine months in, drives fast and says sorry without slowing, taps the wheel at red lights, brings a flask of terrible tea/coffee. Replacing Ijichi (still recovering) as his usual driver. Ijichi had told her Kusakabe doesn't like talking.
- Dispatch board (office by the car yard): overflowing onto taped sheets of printer paper nearly to the floor; a sheet headed "Colonies. Personnel. Do not discuss." lists Panda and Maki (they have gone into the Game; never named which colony). Board top line from 21 Dec in red: "24 Dec. Shinjuku." Coordinator (headset): "They agreed it. Gojo-san and the other one. Like a dentist's appointment." / "One year. Since the Parade. To the day. Did you notice?"
- Okabe's "Interim Guidance" sheet: barriers called colonies, ten across the country, two in Tokyo; "staff who are not players" must not cross into one.
- What Kusakabe knows of the Game: people across the country woke with techniques; some are very old sorcerers in other people's bodies by an old arrangement with "the man in the robes"; points for killing, join or the technique kills you. Ogawa: "Some of ours. The young ones mostly. The first-years. The Okkotsu boy's back."
- Yuji: Kusakabe heard he was dead (a Thursday), then not dead and gone into a barrier (the following Monday), both from Ogawa. "Doesn't know which one he was hoping for. He's careful not to find out."
- Gojo released Wednesday 19 December (radio: "Prison Realm. Confirmed. Repeat, Gojo-san is."). Vague rumour the same night: "something else came loose", "something with the King of Curses. A cost." No details (keep it that way).
- Rest rule: twelve days on, one off, from 2 December, blocked out in Okabe's yellow highlighter. Kusakabe's rest day was a Tuesday.
- Jobs: Adachi hospital boiler-room curse feeding on the evacuation (one cut); Kita school-gym evacuation centre, 300 sleepers, a flat grey curse lying on chests (two cuts, 2:10 a.m.); also Nerima, Itabashi, Ota (two), a laundrette in Nerima on 21 Dec.
- Sister: he now visits Sundays, Wednesdays, sometimes Mondays/Fridays. Ono unplugged her TV ("Told her it's broken") because the news upset her. Ono's cousin in Nakano was evacuated by bus at two hours' notice. Ono's notebook: "If move: call brother. He answers." (underlined); later each page ends "Brother came." He fixed the second window lock with a screwdriver and folded card. Sister: "You're checking on me." / "Like you're counting the windows." She kept her good hand on his sleeve all evening.
- Panda passed him in the corridor outside dispatch on the last Monday of November, carrying a bag: "Sensei." / "Panda." No more. Kusakabe still hasn't sat on the third step since Ch 21. Distance unresolved.
- Kitchen calendar: free paper calendar from Midori Home Care (came with an invoice last December, "Season's Greetings from all the team"), a photo a month, mostly mountains; January was a mountain with a small red shrine; April a lake with blossom; December a snowy bridge with two small people under an umbrella. Never turned until 21 Dec. He circled 24 December (a Monday) in blue with a bank biro and left the pen beside it.
