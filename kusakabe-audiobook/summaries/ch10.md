# Chapter 10. Two Tired Men: summary

A Thursday night in October 2018. Dispatch pairs Kusakabe with Nanami Kento (both Grade 1) on a vacant former freight-company tower in Shinagawa, where a curse manifests after midnight. Nanami is in the lobby first: "Four minutes." The night guard Iwata tells them about fourteen (Accounts, famous for all-nighters) and a colleague found typing on a bare keyboard until his fingers bled. They wait in the fourteenth-floor break room by a lit vending machine. Nanami, past his hours, objects to overtime on principle, buys dreadful coffee, says "Labour is shit", shares half a sandwich from his favourite bakery, and mentions an earnest first-year he's been looking after; Kusakabe changes the subject. Nanami describes Kuantan, the sea, and his unread books. Kusakabe says "Nothing." At 2:11 the phones ring; Kusakabe holds the curse in the circle and Nanami, in Overtime, ends it with one 7:3 strike. At three, Kusakabe admits he has a sister he sees on Sundays. At dawn Nanami reties his tie: "See you."

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Date: a Thursday night into Friday, October 2018 (early-to-mid month). Job sheet: "Kusakabe A. (Grade 1). Nanami K. (Grade 1). Shinagawa. Commercial premises, vacant. Manifests after midnight. Possible Grade 1." Kusakabe took the train; arrived 9:04 p.m.
- Building: twenty-two floors, owned by a freight company until August (moved to Saitama, building for sale). Fourteenth floor = Accounts, notorious for lights on all night; grey partitions, beige unplugged phones (about forty), a break room with a sink, round table, four chairs and a still-lit vending machine. Phone line cut in August.
- Iwata: night guard, sixties, oversized uniform jacket, reading glasses on a cord, eleven years at the building; won't go above the tenth floor after midnight. Bowed deeply at the end; will tell Fukuda's mother.
- Fukuda: young guard, found on fourteen at 3:30 a.m. a fortnight earlier typing on a lone keyboard, fingers raw; alive, remembers nothing. His mother rang Iwata to say thank you.
- The curse: born of the floor's overtime; felt "tired", not angry. Manifested 2:11 a.m. (all phones ringing, sound of typing): a huge stooped grey back with a hundred thin arms ending in typing fingers, no face. Kusakabe cut arms in the circle; Nanami struck the 7:3 point, low on the left side. Exorcised. Car for Kusakabe at four; Nanami walked to the first train (nineteen minutes).
- Nanami as shown: pale suit, small busy-patterned tie, tinted goggle-like glasses with patterned frames, cleaver-like blunt blade wrapped in cloth, briefcase, watch worn face-inward while waiting; went home at six "every day"; took off his tie and wound it round his right hand for Overtime ("From here, this is overtime."), retied it outside at dawn. Calls Gojo "Gojo-san". Worked "four years" at a company in a building like this. "Someone thanked me once. For something very small." Favourite bakery: small, near a station, opens at seven, owner starts at four; he's gone two years; recommends the breaded sandwich, "The melon bread is overrated." Kusakabe means to go "one Sunday" (he never says he will; this is the seed). Kuantan dream: house near the sea, sell everything that needs a key, over a hundred and forty unread books.
- Nanami mentioned "a student", "one of Gojo-san's first-years", earnest, eats a lot, thanks people; Kusakabe knew it was the boy everyone talks about and changed the subject (to a hundred-yen coin in the machine's return slot). No name, no Sukuna.
- IMPORTANT: Kusakabe told Nanami "I've got a sister. I see her on Sundays." Nanami is now the only person at school who knew. Nanami: "Then don't work Sundays... Everything else they'll take." Last words: "Get some sleep before Sunday, Kusakabe." / "See you."
- Kusakabe went home with a folded square of the sandwich's waxed paper in one coat pocket and the cold hundred-yen coin in the other (available for Ch 17 if wanted).
