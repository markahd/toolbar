# Chapter 5. Days Off: summary

Late November to early December 2017. Okabe makes Kusakabe take five days of expiring leave. After his usual Sunday visit, the sofa eats the week: a square of sunlight crossing the floor, no TV, no words spoken. On Tuesday Mrs. Fujita calls; he watches it ring out. Her text says "Nothing urgent now." He puts off calling back all Tuesday and Wednesday, and ignores an unknown number (the agency) twice. On Thursday he calls. His sister choked on her tea on Tuesday, the doctor came, and Mrs. Fujita has given notice, partly because nobody answered. At Midori Home Care, Mr. Hasegawa explains that the care plan lapsed unsigned. Kusakabe says, "That's on me. I should've called back." He interviews two carers, picks the curt, competent Ono Haruka, opens the first invoice at the higher rate, and says goodbye to Mrs. Fujita. He covers Monday and Tuesday himself and sleeps in the window chair. At dawn his sister wakes, knows him and calls him "Nii-san."

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Okabe gave him a leave printout (eleven unused days; "The office is concerned about the figures"). Leave ran Monday to Friday, end of November.
- Mrs. Fujita quit (last shift the Friday of his leave week). Reasons: her knees, her mother now in Saitama, a new morning job near there (a lady who lives with her daughter), and the unanswered calls ("if it goes wrong, it's just me"). Last words to him: "Answer your phone, Kusakabe-san." He said "Thank you" to her.
- The sister choked on tea (Tuesday); the doctor came at three and will visit again. Now on thickened drinks: a powder in the cupboard turns tea "the texture of thin custard". The doctor may want pills crushed.
- New notebook: same hundred-yen kind with a cartoon rabbit, copied out by Mrs. Fujita for Ono. Kusakabe keeps the old cat notebook.
- Midori Home Care: second floor, between a dry cleaner (steam smell in the stairwell) and a shop that sells only umbrellas. One room, four desks, a whiteboard rota with coloured magnets. Policy: when a lead carer leaves, the care plan goes back for review and must be signed by family before new staff can be placed.
- Mr. Hasegawa: sixties, thin, grey cardigan, glasses, mask under his chin, serves green tea that is much too strong.
- Candidate not hired: Mrs. Kubo, forties, round-faced, three-coloured scarf, warm and asks everything. Kusakabe told her he teaches "P.E.".
- Ono Haruka: worked two years in a care home in Adachi, before that a hospital ward assistant. Black puffer jacket kept on, hair pulled back hard, watch face on the inside of her wrist. Never smiled. Starts Wednesday at eight (green magnet). Knows the "count to three" tea rule (wrote "three" on her hand) and the rule about not moving the sister from the window ("I don't do that.").
- Weekends stayed covered; the Monday and Tuesday slots were lost. Kusakabe took those two days off work as "unscheduled" ("Family." to the dispatch coordinator with the headset), and two jobs went to someone else.
- He has a left shoe with a failing heel, still not repaired.
- The sister called him "the new one" and noticed he's "slower than the other lady"; at dawn she called him "Nii-san" and said "You'll do your back in. In my chair."
