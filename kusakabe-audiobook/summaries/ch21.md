# Chapter 21. Execute the Boy: summary

Mid-to-late November 2018. At headquarters, Inspector Sakaguchi takes Kusakabe's witness account of Sukuna on Meiji-dori, then records Grade 1 witnesses' views on Yuji's reinstated death sentence (lapsed "later" now Gojo is sealed). Kusakabe says yes, "Soon. Before it happens again." Walking afterwards, he sees the argument is standing on the same cold fear as B5F, in a better coat, and keeps his view anyway. The dispatch coordinator tells him Okkotsu, now special grade, has been flown back and given the job; the boy hasn't come back from Shibuya. Kusakabe feels relief it isn't him, and finds he can't picture Yuji's face. The courtyard ginkgo drops its leaves. Maki, burned and leaning on her staff, asks him to sign a deferral request; he refuses: "That it should be quick." Maki throws Masaru's "tidier" back at him; he can't answer. Weeks of distance follow. Panda stops sitting near him; Inumaki passes him without a word. He sits alone on the steps.
