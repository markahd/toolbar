# Chapter 20. Gakuganji: summary

A Tuesday in mid-November 2018. In the half-empty staff room, Ogawa tells Kusakabe that it's done: Gakuganji found Yaga out in the hills, in the trees, early that morning. Okabe, in a new black cardigan, hands out a three-line notice and says Yaga "always signed in the right box." On his third step in the empty yard, Kusakabe checks whether he could have stopped it and finds nobody could, not with Gojo sealed. He can't make himself hate Gakuganji. The debt-coin is lighter but not gone: "We're closer to even, old man." He feels tired respect for Yaga. Panda, in hiding, sits on the bottom step; Yaga had promised to teach him to sew "when I was older." Kusakabe stays until Panda leaves. A felt catalogue sits in Yaga's pigeonhole, then disappears. Nobody questions him. On Sunday his sister asks after "the nice principal"; he doesn't tell her. Tea too weak. At dusk she hums a song he doesn't know.
