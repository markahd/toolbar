# Chapter 19. The Corridor: summary

Early November 2018, five days after Shibuya. At headquarters Okabe, in a suit jacket instead of a cardigan, hands Kusakabe an "Account of Movements, 31 October" form. He fills it in, leaving out Tanabe's B5F warning. Walking to the east stairs, he passes two officials: Yaga has been sentenced to death for conspiring with Geto, because he alone can make autonomous cursed corpses with their own souls; he walked out before the arrest order reached him; Panda is sealed downstairs as bait and, if Yaga doesn't come, will be "opened up". Kusakabe nods "Afternoon" and keeps walking. Home through a changed city, he sits on the sofa in his coat. "Not my business. Not my fight." An hour and a half. Then the slab, Panda's face, Takeru on her lap, the debt. "Damn it." He goes out. Next morning Ogawa texts that the bear is out and nobody knows who. Okabe rings a list; "Home." Kusakabe rinses the cup, puts it upside down. His hands are steady.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Date: five days after Shibuya (about 5 November 2018); the freeing happens that night; the morning scene is the next day. The exact method and place are never shown or hinted at.
- Post-Shibuya "Account of Movements, 31 October" statements, due to "the Office" by five; written in Room six, second floor, headquarters (flask of bad tea, whiteboard ghost diagram). About thirty forms in the tray on Okabe's desk. Kusakabe wrote "Ordered" three times, "Withdrew" once, "Tanabe (Grade 2)", "Radio. Fragments." (omitting Tanabe's B5F warning: a lie by omission), "Frozen (ice), duration unknown. Released by special grade Yuki Tsukumo." and left Further Remarks empty. Panda's name is in every box.
- His left hand now has white clinic tape (the cape strip is gone); a dressing on the right forearm.
- Okabe wore a dark buttoned suit jacket, no cardigan; looks unslept; "I'm told everyone's is late. This week." Next morning he rang Kusakabe at about twenty to seven, working through "a list": "Where were you last night? From about six." "Home." "Is there anyone who can confirm that?" "No." "It's a long list." Okabe rang off first (a first).
- The two officials (unnamed): an old man with close grey hair and a dark wool coat over his arm, and a younger man about forty with a cardboard file box. Content: sentence "signed this morning", unanimous, death, for conspiring with Geto Suguru; the fear is the method (cursed corpses with their own souls, "an army that doesn't need paying"); Yaga "walked out of his own office" before the arrest order reached him (Friday's meeting mentioned); Panda held "downstairs, in the old rooms, sealed" as bait; if Yaga doesn't come, Panda is "evidence" to be opened up.
- Kusakabe's private fear: Takeru is exactly the kind of thing they're hunting; staying unremarkable protects his sister. Reprimand has seven months left.
- Headquarters lift out of order (handwritten sign); east stairs; guard he didn't know at the gate.
- City: the line through Shibuya suspended; missing-person photographs on lamp posts; "Water: two per customer"; people looking up at the sky; TV helicopter footage of Shibuya like a quarry.
- Ogawa's text: "bear's out. last night sometime. nobody knows who. upstairs going mad" (Ogawa doesn't use punctuation).
- Kusakabe left his lamp on when he went out. His flat door lock sticks if the key is turned too fast. Someone upstairs drags a chair.
- Kusakabe has told no one, will never discuss it. Panda's whereabouts after release are not stated ("That's Panda's business now").
