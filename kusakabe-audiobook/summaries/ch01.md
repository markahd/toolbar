# Chapter 1. Six Ten: summary

Late October 2017. Kusakabe wakes at 6:10, checks the unreported water stain and drinks bad instant coffee at the window, watching a salaryman freeze at a crossing. He leaves the mug in the sink, walks the long way past the shrine ginkgos, and reaches the staff car yard 26 minutes late. Ijichi drives him to a probable Grade 2 in a half-empty apartment block due for demolition, where the tenants hear knocking, always three times. Circle, blade, done in eleven minutes. An old tenant takes him for the gas inspector. He says it was the pipes, and she shuts the window without thanking him. Back at school, the administrator Okabe asks why last week's Saitama report is late. "No." Okabe notes it on his file. Evening: a convenience-store bento, the TV on but unwatched, a text from Mrs. Fujita. He sends the reports at 11:04, rinses the mug and puts it upside down.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Landlord: Mr. Sato (the stain is "above the wardrobe", "roughly the shape of a country he's never been to"; he's meant to report it "for two years").
- The 6:10 alarm: he set it years ago on the theory that ten extra minutes would make him a different person. It didn't.
- His walk to school: 25 minutes. He takes the long way, behind a shrine (ginkgo trees) and up the hill through the trees, to avoid a crowded crossing. He goes in by the side way, past the storehouse and bins, to the staff car yard.
- Ijichi drives a black sedan. He is anxious, careful and polite, and apologises to other drivers. Kusakabe doesn't mind him.
- Okabe: administration, forties, neat side parting, cardigan "the colour of weak tea", clipboard, highlights late reports in yellow. He noted the late Saitama warehouse report (the assignment was the Tuesday before; the report was due Friday at five) on Kusakabe's record. Kusakabe sent it at 11:04 that night.
- Saitama warehouse job: a security guard who had been living in the back office was present, unharmed, and kept saying sorry. Kusakabe's report says only "Referred to local services."
- Mrs. Fujita texts him ("Sunday as usual? She had a good day today. Ate all her lunch."). He replies "Yes. Thanks."
- Yaga seen only in passing (on his phone in a corridor). No debt reflection yet; that's Ch 2.
- First-years heard but not seen (a girl's sharp voice; a deep voice: "Ow, okay, fair."). Kusakabe doesn't look.
- Kusakabe's back clicks when he stands up.
