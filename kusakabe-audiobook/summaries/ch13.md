# Chapter 13. Halloween: summary

31 October 2018. Kusakabe and Panda are on crowd standby at the north end of Shibuya, near Meiji-Jingumae. Panda, taken for a costume, poses for photos; a witch asks Kusakabe what he's meant to be: "A teacher." At 19:00 a vast curtain drops over the station. Orders arrive in fragments: dropped calls, contradictory texts, radio scraps. Trapped civilians chant "Bring Gojo Satoru" without knowing who he is. At 20:14 Gojo goes in alone. Ijichi sends them down the Fukutoshin tunnel south. Past the outer curtain they cut through small curses, find trapped civilians in a service passage, break an inner curtain keyed against sorcerers by cutting a curse user's talisman and two fingers, and fight up an escalator. His fear rises: ankles, knees, waist, chest, throat. Inside, the curtain overhead is a starless black sky.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Job sheet: "Standby. Shibuya area (north). 31 Oct. 18:00 until crowds disperse. Kusakabe A. Panda (second year)." The school posts people on big crowd nights. Five standby points round the station, a pair at each. Ijichi drove them in at 17:30 and mentioned "unusual readings. Earlier in the week" that weren't put on the sheet.
- The rest of his homeroom is "on other people's lists tonight" (Inumaki and Maki not on the page).
- Panda calls Yaga "Masamichi" ("Masamichi would hate this"). Panda was given a small plastic pumpkin of sweets (orange chews that taste of soap), which he later gave to a hurt girl in a cat costume.
- Timeline used: curtain dropped at 19:00 over the station (canon) (about 400 m radius). Civilians can't get out; sorcerers can pass. People pressed against it chanted "Bring Gojo Satoru." Dispatch texts: "ALL STAFF HOLD POSITION. DO NOT ENTER. FURTHER ORDERS TO FOLLOW." then "Disregard. Await team assignment." At 20:14 the radios reported Gojo had gone into the station alone. Ijichi's orders: inner curtains layered "like a set of boxes", some keyed against sorcerers, held by curse users on upper floors and at exits; teams to take them down. Kusakabe and Panda: from Meiji-Jingumae, down into the subway, follow the Fukutoshin line south.
- Panda had an auxiliary's radio clipped to his collar. Kusakabe's phone has no signal inside.
- Route: empty, lit Meiji-Jingumae station; a train standing with doors open; along the tunnel, most of a kilometre, through the outer curtain (it "gives like the skin on cold milk"). Swarm of small flat many-legged curses on the tunnel roof (Grade 3 or 4). A service door to a concrete passage with 20 to 30 civilians who'd tried to walk out along the tracks: a man in a gorilla suit holding the head, a girl dressed as a cartoon cat with a cut forehead, a woman in office clothes. Inner curtain across the stairs at the half landing. Curse user: young man, twenties, hooded jacket with a skull, holding a written paper strip; Kusakabe cut the strip and two of his fingers; Panda knocked him out; alive. The stairs came up in the basement of a shopping building (mannequins in Halloween capes), escalator to the street held by three or four curses, one big. They are now inside the big curtain, on a street north of the station.
- Injuries: a nick on the back of his left hand (tunnel); a shallow cut across his forearm "the same arm as last time" (escalator). Panda has something dark spiked in the fur of one shoulder. Panda offered a paw twice (track bed, top of the escalator) and Kusakabe refused it both times, getting up by himself. Taking the paw is saved for Ch 16.
- B5F not yet mentioned. Gojo not seen on the page.
