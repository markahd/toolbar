# Chapter 12. Rumours: summary

June to late October 2018, as secondhand news. In June the Grade 2 Ogawa tells Kusakabe a Sendai schoolboy ate a Sukuna finger and lived. Sentenced to death, Itadori Yuji, fifteen, is spared by Gojo until he has eaten all twenty, and enrols. Kusakabe glimpses pink hair and hides in an empty classroom. In July Ijichi tells him Sukuna tore out the boy's heart; Kusakabe feels relief. Gojo carries rental DVDs. Kanagawa is only a sealed file with Nanami's name. Sent to Ibaraki during the Goodwill Event, he returns to a line cut through the woods. Panda: Yuji is alive, came out of a box. Inumaki tore his throat on the special grade. Rumours: Todo, stolen vault items, Kyoto's principal ordering the boy killed. Gojo: "I'm here." In October Kusakabe avoids Yuji, changes route once in the rain (Maki notices), and turns away from a laughing pink-haired boy in the courtyard.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- **Ogawa**: Grade 2, late fifties, at the school "longer than the radiators", teaches basic warding, unused toothpick he moves from side to side, knows everything first ("doesn't go home"). Whispers the ugly rumour and looks at the door.
- Kusakabe first heard of Yuji on a wet Tuesday in June (same month as Ch 9; he was filling in Maki's eligibility form).
- He glimpsed Yuji once in June (pink hair, hooded uniform, laughing beside Gojo) and hid in an empty classroom reading an April timetable; his circle came out unbidden.
- Early July: Ijichi, driving to Chiba, told him about the detention centre ("I drove them"; first-years sent three weeks in; Gojo was away; Gojo said he'd talk to Ijichi "later"). Kusakabe felt relief at the death. He has not told anyone.
- August: Gojo at the gate with a rental-shop bag of DVDs ("Film night").
- Kanagawa (Junpei/Mahito) reached him only as: a school, an assembly hall, a curse that changes people's shapes, Nanami's name on a sealed file.
- Goodwill Event: Kusakabe was NOT there; dispatched to a dam overflow tunnel in Ibaraki (curse cut in the dark, water round his ankles). Driven back by a young unnamed female auxiliary; arrived after it was over. Saw the straight line cut through the woods behind the school (Gojo).
- Heard: Yuji alive, jumped out of a box Gojo wheeled into the meeting and said surprise; Kugisaki cried and pretended she didn't; Todo calls Yuji "brother"; curse users took sealed things (wombs, fingers) from the vaults; the curtain kept Gojo out for a while; day two was baseball, Tokyo won; Kyoto's principal told his students to kill the boy. Inumaki used Cursed Speech on the special grade and coughed blood (fresh white dressing under his jaw; rattled his lozenge tin, half full, as a report). Maki fought her sister and said nothing; hit him harder that afternoon. Panda: missing fur patches, bandaged ear; "He's fun. You'd like him." / "Doubt it." / "You haven't met him." / "Let's keep it that way."
- Gojo told him Yuji threw a shot put "halfway across a stadium" at his old school.
- Ogawa covered a theory lesson: Yuji asked where curses go after exorcism; "nowhere"; Yuji said that was sad.
- Sunday in October: he checked his sister's window lock twice; "You always were a worrier."
- The route change: one rainy late-October morning he avoided the covered walkway past the first-years' door and went round outside past the storehouse, five minutes, soaked. Maki: "You're wet." / "Rain." (She saw the walkway was dry.) Panda watched. He never does it that way again; he "gets better at timing."
- He has never seen Yuji's face up close, doesn't know his eye colour or voice when not laughing. Never met or spoke to him in Ch 12.
- Courtyard in front of the old building has a ginkgo that drops all its leaves in one night each year (not yet dropped at end of October).
