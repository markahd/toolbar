# Chapter 15. Stay Still: summary

31 October 2018, about 22:00, inside the curtain. Walking south down Meiji-dori towards the regroup point, three metres behind Panda, Kusakabe hears radio fragments that Gojo has been "sealed"; asked, he says "Don't know", and it's true. A little after ten the air goes heavy and his circle comes up on its own. He senses two presences: a hot, ancient, proud one south-west beyond the station, and one with no edge that bends everything towards it. Panda recognises it as coming from Yuji, but not Yuji. Fire lights the curtain. Kusakabe orders an honest retreat ("I'm scared. That's the first reason.") and they gather six people from a hat-shop doorway. The fire stops; the hot presence vanishes. Sukuna, in Yuji's burnt uniform, drops into Meiji-dori: "Don't move." Kusakabe becomes furniture. The four eyes pass through him. Gone. His hand won't leave the sword.

## Continuity updates

**New names and facts**
- Times: walking south from about 21:50; the pressure starts "a little after ten". Kusakabe and Panda never reach the crossing below the station; they turn back north on Meiji-dori (uphill) on Kusakabe's order. They disobeyed the regroup order.
- Radio fragments: "Gojo-san has been", "no, sealed, she said sealed", "that's not possible, say again". Nobody confirms. Kusakabe: "Gojo doesn't get sealed. Gojo is what you put on top of everything else so it stays shut." He does not know for certain that Gojo is sealed.
- Jogo is never named; Kusakabe senses him only as the hot, angry, ancient, proud presence to the south-west, "up the hill" beyond the station, and feels it vanish ("a hole the shape of it, cooling"). He infers, without being told, that it died.
- Panda recognised Sukuna's energy as coming "from where Yuji would be" but not Yuji ("It's the other one."). Neither says the name Sukuna aloud.
- Kusakabe's retreat speech, his longest of the night: "I'm scared. That's the first reason. The second reason is there's nothing down there we can do... That's what we're for." Panda: "Okay. Yeah. Okay." Kusakabe has still NOT confessed the B5F lie.
- Civilians gathered from the doorway of a shop that sells only hats, in a side street off Meiji-dori (bars, a hair salon): a man in a security jacket with an unused torch; two girls in matching sailor costumes holding hands; a woman in a bed-sheet ghost costume; an old man in ordinary clothes; a young male auxiliary in a black suit who keeps switching his radio on and off ("Someone said wait here. I don't know who."). Six people. Panda took them north to a van at the top of Meiji-dori.
- Sukuna as seen: about thirty metres off, dropped "out of the dark" into the middle of Meiji-dori by a burning bin; Yuji's body, first-year uniform mostly torn and burnt, bare arms, pink hair pushed back, black brush-like markings across the cheeks and brow, a second pair of eyes under the first, four red eyes. Lines: "Don't move." / "I'm not done. I don't want to be bothered. Stay where you are." His eyes paused briefly on Panda, then passed through Kusakabe. He vanished without a visible step, heading south-west.
- Kusakabe kept the circle up the whole time (afraid that dropping it would count as moving) and never drew his sword "not an inch". Panda stood rigid, paw clenched, and did not call Yuji's name.
- Afterwards: Panda: "That was Yuji's face." / "No. It wasn't." / "He's not in there right now. Is he." (no answer). Kusakabe has still never seen Yuji's own face up close.
- Kusakabe stays behind alone ("In a minute.") at the mouth of the side street. The bandage on his left knuckles (Ch 14) is bleeding through again. Ends standing alone in the empty street, right hand white-knuckled on the hilt. (Ch 16 opens with the collision.)
