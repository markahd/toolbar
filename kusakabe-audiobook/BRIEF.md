# BRIEF: "The Circle That Doesn't Need a Name", a Kusakabe audio fan fiction

This is the complete creative brief. Everything creative has already been decided. Writers execute; they do not invent beyond what is allowed below.

---

## 0. Targets at a glance

- **Length:** about **100,000 words** total (about 10–12 hours of audio).
- **Chapters:** **25** (24 main chapters of about 4,000–4,300 words, plus a shorter epilogue of about 2,000 words).
- **Point of view:** close third person on Kusakabe, **present tense**, with frequent short inner thoughts.
- **Style:** exactly like the **Style Reference** in Appendix A. Read it before anything else. Every chapter must read like it was written by the same hand.
- **Narration:** one narrator voice for the whole book (Fish Audio Drama 3 via OpenRouter, default **Sarah** voice; Drama 3 reads anything in square brackets as a stage direction, so the TTS text must contain none). Write for the ear.
- **Output per chapter:** two files:
  - `chNN.md`: the reading version (italics allowed for inner thoughts).
  - `chNN_tts.txt`: the audio version (plain text, cleaned for text-to-speech; see section 6).

---

## 1. What this story is (and isn't)

**It is:** Kusakabe's real, tired, ordinary life as a Grade 1 sorcerer with no innate technique, inside the canon timeline. Missions, students, paperwork, his sister, money, avoidance, days that collapse into nothing, small kindnesses he'd deny, bad decisions, consequences, people who are good to him, people who aren't, people who don't notice him. And, a few times, the moments he *can't* avoid, where his body moves before his fear gets a vote.

**It is not:**
- An action novel. Fights are short, sensory and mostly told through what he feels and notices. Never long blow-by-blow choreography.
- A childhood story. **No childhood flashbacks. No origin backstory.** Everything happens in his adult present.
- A power fantasy. He never gains new abilities, never makes heroic speeches, never becomes someone else.
- The Sukuna fight. **The Shinjuku battle is never shown on the page.** The story stops at dawn as he walks out the door, then jumps to the epilogue.

**The emotional engine:** Kusakabe avoids almost everything, and it mostly works, until it doesn't. The world comes for him anyway. The book is about the gap between what he avoids and the few things that are already inside his circle before he can decide, and about slowly realising that his small circle can be enough without being a hiding place.

**Hidden purpose (never stated):** the reader relates to Kusakabe: introverted, tired, avoids confrontation and being questioned, hates explaining himself, built everything without inheritance, works for money that goes to other people's needs, lets days off collapse into nothing. The story should quietly show **how he carries himself**: the outer face versus the inner voice, one-line answers, owning things briefly, stepping in when it's his, letting go when it isn't. **Never explain or teach any of this.** Show it through what he does and what happens.

## 2. Canon rules (do not break)

Kusakabe and every canon scene stay **exactly as canon has them**. You add around canon, never change it.

**Fixed canon facts about Kusakabe:**
- Grade 1 sorcerer at Tokyo Jujutsu High. **No innate cursed technique.** Uses a katana and the **New Shadow Style**, especially **Simple Domain**: a small circle around himself in which he senses anything that enters and reacts automatically.
- Personality: laid-back, tired-looking, complains often, self-preserving, honest about fear, quietly responsible, gentle underneath. Colleagues see him as down to earth and reliable despite the complaining.
- He has a **younger sister**. Her son **Takeru** died young in an accident. The trauma left her with memory loss and paralysis. Kusakabe looks after her care.
- **Principal Masamichi Yaga** (creator of Panda) used his cursed-corpse technique to give Takeru's soul an independent cursed-corpse form, so mother and son could be reunited. Kusakabe owes Yaga an enormous debt and keeps this secret completely.
- In 2018 he is the **homeroom teacher of the second-years: Maki Zen'in, Panda and Toge Inumaki.** (Yuta Okkotsu is abroad that year.)

**Canon events to include, in this order** (details in the chapter plan):
1. **Night Parade of a Hundred Demons** (24 December 2017): Kusakabe is among the sorcerers fighting curses in **Shinjuku**.
2. **Shibuya Incident** (31 October 2018):
   - Posted with **Panda** in Shibuya. When special-grade curses are reported on **B5F**, Kusakabe **lies to Panda** to keep them both away from that floor, out of fear.
   - He senses **Sukuna** clashing with **Jogo**, backs off, and when **Sukuna appears and orders everyone not to move**, he stands absolutely still.
   - **After the collision** (the devastation from Sukuna's fighting), he lies **trapped under a slab of rock, holding his katana, lost**, until **Panda lifts the rock off him.**
   - **Inumaki is badly injured in Shibuya and loses his left arm** (Kusakabe learns of it afterwards).
   - **Nanami dies in Shibuya** (Kusakabe hears afterwards).
   - **Kenjaku** (in Geto's body) unleashes **Maximum: Uzumaki**. Kusakabe **intercepts and deflects it to protect Kasumi Miwa** (a New Shadow Style practitioner). Kenjaku is genuinely impressed that someone of this generation still uses real sorcery.
   - During Kenjaku's fight with Choso, **Uraume freezes Kusakabe**. **Yuki Tsukumo** rescues him.
3. **After Shibuya:**
   - **Yaga is arrested** and sentenced to death. **Panda is held** to lure him in. **Kusakabe secretly frees Panda**, repaying his debt to Yaga. Never explain the mechanics. It happens offstage and he never talks about it.
   - **Yaga is executed** (carried out by **Gakuganji**).
   - Kusakabe **supports executing Yuji Itadori** quickly, out of fear of Sukuna. Yuta is assigned as executioner. Keep Kusakabe's view and his students' disagreement. Don't dramatise the Yuta–Yuji events beyond what Kusakabe hears.
   - The **Culling Game** begins. Kusakabe is **not a player**. He stays in Tokyo, keeping things running and watching over his sister.
   - In the month before **Shinjuku**, Kusakabe **helps train Yuji**: drilling reaction, footwork and reading attacks. He never apologises for having wanted him dead. He just teaches him properly.
4. **Shinjuku Showdown** begins **24 December 2018**, one year to the day after the Night Parade. **Do not show the battle.** The last main chapter ends with him walking out at dawn.
5. **Epilogue:** he survived, with two long scars across his chest, and became **head of the New Shadow Style** (its old binding vows lifted). A Sunday with his sister. Don't describe the fight. He can refer to it only vaguely ("bit of work").

**Fan-invented material is allowed only where canon is silent:** ordinary missions, side characters (curse victims, a carer, a clan official, a nurse, a landlord), his flat, his routines, conversations between canon events. It must never contradict canon or put him somewhere canon places him elsewhere.

**Canon characters who may appear, true to canon:** Gojo, Yaga, Maki, Panda, Inumaki, Ijichi (auxiliary manager and driver), Nanami (one fan scene before Shibuya), Miwa, Yuki, Kenjaku, Uraume, Sukuna (only the Shibuya "stay still" moment), Yuji, Yuta (offstage, or a brief canon-consistent appearance), Gakuganji (offstage). **Takeru:** mention him only in the canon terms above. Never show what happens to Takeru's cursed corpse after Yaga's death. Leave it out completely.

## 3. Voice and style rules (match Appendix A exactly)

- **Present tense, close third person.** Short sentences. Lots of white space. Scene breaks between scenes.
- **Inner thoughts** are short, dry, honest and often funny. In the reading version use *italics*. In the TTS version, write them plainly and make them clear by ear (see section 6).
- **Tone:** tired, deadpan, self-deprecating, quietly tender. Humour comes from understatement. ("*Grade 1 sorcerer. One of the strongest in the country, apparently. Can't be bothered to email the landlord.*")
- **Sparse dialogue.** He speaks in one line where others use a paragraph. He never explains himself. ("You're late." / "Yeah." / "Why?" / "Traffic.")
- **Recurring motifs** to use with restraint: the **water stain** on his ceiling he never reports; **bad instant coffee**; the **circle** (Simple Domain) as body sense and as his life; the **debt** to Yaga, felt like a coin in his pocket; **Sundays** with his sister (lavender smell, tea made too weak the way she likes it); the **sofa** and the days that disappear into nothing; *shikata ga nai*, used rarely and only at real turning points.
- **Show consequences.** Avoiding things costs him something real. Facing things sometimes costs something too. Earlier choices ripple forward where the plan says so.
- **Other people are real.** Some are good to him, some are difficult, many don't notice him at all. Not everyone warms up. Nobody exists only to teach him something.
- **No therapy-speak, no self-help phrasing, no stated morals.** Never end a chapter on a lesson. End on an image, an action or a small dry thought.
- **Action scenes:** brief, physical, sensory, and told through his body and fear. A curse, the circle, the blade, then the aftermath. Never more than a few hundred words of fighting at a time.
- **Vary texture:** some chapters span one afternoon, some a fortnight. Some are mostly silence, some mostly conversation.
- **Never use the phrase "Simple Domain" more than a few times per chapter.** Usually just "the circle."

## 4. The chapter plan (follow exactly)

Each chapter lists: **time**, **canon anchors** (must match), **beats** (write these), **conflict and consequence**, and the **closing image**. Approximate word counts are in brackets.

### PART ONE: THE MAN WHO DIDN'T WANT THE JOB (late 2017)

**Ch 1. Six Ten** [4,200]
- *Time:* autumn 2017.
- *Beats:* 6:10 alarm, the flat, the water stain, instant coffee at the window watching salarymen (envy of ordinary problems). Walk to school, late. Ijichi drives him to a Grade 2 mission at an abandoned apartment block where tenants keep hearing knocking: the circle, the blade, done efficiently, no glory. Back at school, a mid-level official asks why his mission report from last week is late. He answers in one line and offers nothing more. The official is annoyed and Kusakabe lets him be annoyed. Evening alone: TV on, not watching it.
- *Conflict and consequence:* the late report gets noted on his file. Small, but it's there.
- *Closing image:* he rinses the cup and puts it upside down on the rack. "*Another day of pretending I'm not tired.*"

**Ch 2. Sunday** [4,200]
- *Time:* the next Sunday.
- *Beats:* his sister's flat: lavender, tea too weak, the window chair. Some days she knows him, some days she calls him by their father's name and he answers to it. The carer's routine, the bills he pays without telling anyone. Reflection in the adult present (no childhood scenes): Takeru's death, her breaking, him taking it on. Yaga's gift, Takeru given form again, a child on his mother's lap. The debt, like a coin he checks. On the train home: "*I owe him everything. And I don't even like him that much.*"
- *Conflict and consequence:* the carer mentions her rates are going up. He says "Fine" without checking his finances. He'll feel it next month.
- *Closing image:* the train window, his reflection over the city lights. He looks tired, and for once he doesn't look away.

**Ch 3. The Hurricane with Sunglasses** [4,200]
- *Beats:* Gojo ambushes him in a corridor and talks him into covering a mission at a hospital: a curse growing in a children's ward, fed by fear. Kusakabe tries three ways to say no and ends up saying yes without knowing how. The hospital: the smell, the small beds, a tired night nurse who's curt with him. He handles it without taking it personally. The curse is ugly and fast. Circle, blade, done. Afterwards, a small unseen kindness: he quietly fixes a broken night light in a child's room. Back at school, Gojo praises him loudly in front of others, "best Grade 1 without a technique," and Kusakabe feels the weight of having to live up to it.
- *Conflict and consequence:* the praise means more missions get pushed his way.
- *Closing image:* the night light glowing in an empty corridor as he leaves.

**Ch 4. Paperwork** [4,200]
- *Beats:* a mandatory meeting with the higher-ups. He finds the back door out of it. Consequence: he's assigned a worse mission partnered with a young Grade 2 from a minor branch of a big clan, who openly looks down on him for having no technique. On the mission, the clan kid overreaches and nearly dies. Kusakabe steps in, one move, and saves him. In the report, the kid takes the credit. Kusakabe lets it go and doesn't correct the record.
- *Conflict and consequence:* letting it go costs him. The clan kid's version becomes the official one, and someone higher up now thinks Kusakabe froze.
- *Closing image:* he signs the report under the kid's version without reading it twice.

**Ch 5. Days Off** [4,200]
- *Beats:* a run of days off. The sofa eats them. Not rest, just existing until dark, speaking to no one. A missed call from his sister's carer. He sees it and doesn't call back that day, or the next. When he finally does, the carer is quitting, partly because nobody returned her calls. He has to fix it: phone calls, interviews with new carers, explaining things he hates explaining. He owns his delay in one line to the agency: "That's on me. I should've called back."
- *Conflict and consequence:* a gap in his sister's care for two days, which he covers himself, sleeping in her chair.
- *Closing image:* asleep in the chair by her window at dawn, his sister watching him, recognising him.

**Ch 6. The Night of a Hundred Demons** [4,300]
- *Time:* 24 December 2017.
- *Canon anchors:* Geto declares war and the Night Parade hits Shinjuku and Kyoto. Kusakabe is posted in Shinjuku.
- *Beats:* neon, screaming, curses crawling from alleys. His palms are cold. "*I want to be on my sofa watching a bad drama.*" A curse lunges at a fleeing family and his body moves before the thought finishes. They never look back. Then the methodical night: curse, blade, next. Dawn: sitting on a curb, blood on his sleeve, a can of coffee from a surviving vending machine. He hears that Yuta Okkotsu and Gojo are the heroes of the night.
- *Closing image:* he crushes the can, goes home and sleeps fourteen hours. Nobody thanks him. Nobody needs to.

### PART TWO: THE YEAR BETWEEN (2018)

**Ch 7. Homeroom** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* he becomes homeroom teacher of the second-years, Maki, Panda and Inumaki. Yuta is abroad.
- *Beats:* first lessons. Late. Maki demands why; "Traffic." She knows he walked, and lets it go. That's the arrangement. One sentence of teaching that works ("*Your back heel lifts before you strike.*"). Panda on the grass: "Ever think about quitting?" "Every day." "Why don't you?" "Haven't found anything else that pays like this and lets me complain this much." Not entirely a joke. Inumaki saying "Salmon" to nobody.
- *Conflict and consequence:* Maki pushes him to spar seriously. He refuses. She calls him lazy in front of the others. He takes it. It stings more than he shows.
- *Closing image:* he watches the three of them train from the steps, and for one moment doesn't wish he were somewhere else.

**Ch 8. Rice Ball Words** [4,200]
- *Beats:* a mission with Inumaki, whose Cursed Speech means he talks only in rice-ball ingredients to avoid hurting people. Kusakabe relates: another one who says little. Inumaki strains his voice using his technique to save a civilian. Afterwards, an official interrogates the situation, and Kusakabe has to speak *for* Inumaki: explaining, defending, the exact thing he hates most. He does it, briefly and well.
- *Conflict and consequence:* the official is unsatisfied and files a complaint. Inumaki writes "thanks" on his phone screen and shows it to him. Kusakabe nods and says nothing.
- *Closing image:* both of them silent on a bench outside the clinic, Inumaki with a lozenge, Kusakabe with bad coffee. A comfortable silence.

**Ch 9. The Clan Problem** [4,200]
- *Beats:* a visiting Zen'in official belittles Maki for having no cursed energy. Kusakabe sees himself in it: no technique, never wanted by anyone with power. His instinct is to stay out of it. Then the official says one thing too many. Kusakabe says one calm line that ends the conversation. Not a speech.
- *Conflict and consequence:* he's formally reprimanded for disrespecting a clan representative. Maki never mentions it, but sparring with him becomes her quiet way of saying thank you.
- *Closing image:* Maki tossing him a practice sword. "Again." He sighs. "Fine."

**Ch 10. Two Tired Men** [4,200]
- *Fan scene, before Shibuya:* a joint mission or a long wait with **Nanami Kento**. Two exhausted adults who don't believe in heroics, doing the job properly anyway. Bad vending-machine coffee. Talk about overtime, about how labour is a kind of trap, about what they'd do if they could stop. Nanami speaks of quiet things he'd like one day. Kusakabe admits nothing, then admits one thing. Mutual respect without saying so.
- *Conflict and consequence:* none on the surface. Seed the loss: this is the last time they'll talk.
- *Closing image:* Nanami adjusting his tie and walking off. "See you." Kusakabe raises a hand without looking.

**Ch 11. The Offer** [4,200]
- *Beats:* a wealthy family's private security firm offers him a job: much more money, far less danger, no students, no higher-ups. The perfect doorway. He thinks about his sister's care costs. He nearly says yes. He second-guesses it for days: late-night lists, recalculating. He finally turns it down, because of the debt to Yaga and because the school is close to his sister. Then at 11pm he reopens the question, and finally makes himself stop: "*Decided. Done. No more.*"
- *Conflict and consequence:* the money would have solved real problems. It stays a real loss. The story doesn't pretend otherwise.
- *Closing image:* he deletes the recruiter's number, then stares at the ceiling stain.

**Ch 12. Rumours** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* Yuji Itadori, Sukuna's vessel, has enrolled. Events of the year reach Kusakabe secondhand: Yuji's "death" and return, the attack during the Kyoto Goodwill Event (he's not present, he hears about it). Keep it consistent with canon and offstage.
- *Beats:* his unease grows. Sukuna inside a boy, at his school. He avoids meeting Yuji, keeps his distance in corridors, changes his route once. Talk among teachers. Gojo's confidence. His fear, quiet and steady.
- *Conflict and consequence:* his avoidance means he never gets a sense of who Yuji actually is, which will matter later.
- *Closing image:* across a courtyard, a pink-haired boy laughing with friends. Kusakabe turns and takes the long way round.

### PART THREE: SHIBUYA (31 October 2018)

**Ch 13. Halloween** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* Shibuya sealed under curtains. Gojo goes in. Sorcerers deployed. Kusakabe is posted with Panda near Shibuya, with others.
- *Beats:* crowds in costumes, then the curtain, then chaos. Orders in fragments. Nobody knows what's happening. Curse users and curses on the upper levels. Short, tense skirmishes. His fear rising like water.
- *Closing image:* the curtain overhead like a black sky with no stars.

**Ch 14. B5F** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* special-grade curses reported on B5F. Panda wants to go down. Kusakabe lies to keep them away from that floor.
- *Beats:* the cold sliding feeling in his stomach. The calm lie: "*I heard they've already got people down there.*" Panda's long look. "Okay, sensei." The relief, warm and immediate, then the smaller, colder shame underneath. "*Nobody's going to know.*" He doesn't feel fine. He feels small. They hold their position and fight what comes to them.
- *Conflict and consequence:* the lie sits in him for the rest of the night.
- *Closing image:* Panda's back as he walks ahead. Kusakabe can't look at it for long.

**Ch 15. Stay Still** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* he senses Sukuna and Jogo clashing and backs off. Sukuna appears and orders everyone not to move. He stands absolutely still.
- *Beats:* the pressure in the air, his circle snapping up on its own. An honest retreat. Then *that* presence, eyes that have seen a thousand years of people like him and found them boring. "*Don't breathe too loud. Don't be interesting. Be furniture.*" The King of Curses looks right through him. Gone.
- *Closing image:* his hand on his sword, white-knuckled, long after Sukuna has left.

**Ch 16. Under the Rock** [4,300] (the heart of the book)
- *Canon anchors:* the collision. He ends up under a slab of rock, holding his katana, lost, until **Panda lifts it off him**.
- *Beats:* the world comes down. Dark. Dust in his mouth. A pocket of space under the angled slab. His hand still gripping the katana, which he doesn't remember choosing. He doesn't move or call out. Long inner reckoning: what was any of it for? His sister. Takeru on her lap. Yaga and the unpaid debt. The water stain. The sofa. The days that vanished. "*I avoided everything. And it still found me.*" Avoidance felt like control, and the world came down anyway. Not afraid, just lost. "*Maybe if I lie here long enough, someone else will have dealt with it.*" Then grinding, light, dust, the slab lifting. Panda above him, fur matted with dust and blood: "Sensei. You alive?" The student he lied to, a cursed corpse like Takeru, who didn't have to come looking. "Yeah. I'm alive." A paw held out. He takes it. He almost says *sorry* or *thank you*, and says neither. "Let's go." "Okay, sensei."
- *Closing image:* the two of them walking through grey ruins, Kusakabe a half-step behind.

**Ch 17. The Count** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* news arrives in pieces. Nanami is dead. Inumaki is badly hurt and has lost his left arm. Shibuya is a ruin.
- *Beats:* no tears. Tasks instead: checking on people, carrying stretchers, filling in where needed. Nanami's "See you" from Chapter 10 replaying. Seeing Inumaki, or hearing of him: the boy who says little, now with less. Kusakabe sits with that and says nothing.
- *Closing image:* a vending machine glowing in a dead street. He buys two coffees out of habit, then stands there holding the second one.

**Ch 18. Uzumaki** [4,300]
- *Canon anchors:* Kenjaku (Geto's body) appears. **Maximum: Uzumaki.** Kusakabe intercepts and deflects it to protect Miwa. Kenjaku is impressed. Later, **Uraume freezes him** during Kenjaku's fight with Choso, and **Yuki Tsukumo** rescues him.
- *Beats:* the wrongness of the false Geto. The spiral twisting the air. Miwa frozen in fear. One tenth of a second: no thought, no avoidance, no room for it. He moves. Circle, blade, a lifetime of drilling, and he *turns* the spiral like water around a stone. His arms shaking, hands numb. Kenjaku's interest. Then confusion: "*Why didn't I avoid that?*" The realisation, which he'll only fully reach days later: some things never reach the part of you that decides. They're already inside your circle. Then the ice. "*Save a kid, get turned into an ice sculpture. Very dignified.*" Yuki breaks him out. Dawn over Shibuya.
- *Closing image:* thawing, teeth chattering, he looks at his own hands like they belong to someone braver.

### PART FOUR: AFTER (November–December 2018)

**Ch 19. The Corridor** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* Yaga is arrested and sentenced to death. Panda is held to lure him. **Kusakabe secretly frees Panda.**
- *Beats:* he overhears two officials in a corridor. Keeps walking. Home. Sofa. "*Not my business. Not my fight.*" An hour of sitting. Then the slab, the dark, Panda's face. Takeru on his sister's lap. The debt. "*Damn it.*" He goes out. **Never show how he does it.** Cut to the morning: Panda is free, nobody knows who did it, and Kusakabe is drinking bad coffee at his window as if nothing happened.
- *Conflict and consequence:* real risk. If he's found out, it could end him. He accepts it without drama.
- *Closing image:* the cup rinsed and placed upside down. His hands are steady.

**Ch 20. Gakuganji** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* Yaga is executed, carried out by Gakuganji.
- *Beats:* he couldn't stop it. Nobody could. The debt is lighter but not gone. His feeling about Yaga: tired respect. "*We're closer to even, old man.*" Sunday with his sister. She asks about "the nice principal." He doesn't tell her. Tea, too weak. He keeps her world small and safe.
- *Closing image:* his sister humming at the window. He doesn't know the song. He listens anyway.

**Ch 21. Execute the Boy** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* Kusakabe supports executing Yuji quickly, out of fear of Sukuna. Yuta is assigned as executioner (keep the details offstage and canon-consistent).
- *Beats:* his reasoning: not cruelty, but the same fear that said *avoid B5F*, wearing a more reasonable coat. Maki and Panda disagree with him. There's a real conflict with his own students, short and cold. He doesn't defend himself well. He doesn't try.
- *Conflict and consequence:* distance from his students for weeks. Panda stops coming to sit near him.
- *Closing image:* the training yard, the steps where he usually sits, empty of everyone but him.

**Ch 22. Culling Season** [4,200]
- *Canon anchors:* the Culling Game begins. Kusakabe is not a player. He stays in Tokyo.
- *Beats:* a broken city. He keeps things running, takes the jobs nobody else can, and checks on his sister more often than before. The sofa tries to eat his days again, and this time he gets up. Not out of resolve, just because sitting still has started to feel like being under the rock. News arrives in fragments: the Game, the players, the losses, Gojo's eventual release. The date for Shinjuku is set: 24 December. One year to the day.
- *Closing image:* he marks 24 December on a paper calendar in his kitchen. The first thing he's written on it in a year.

**Ch 23. Training Yuji** [4,300]
- *Canon anchors:* in the month before Shinjuku, Kusakabe helps train Yuji.
- *Beats:* the boy he wanted dead, in front of him in a training yard. No apology. One sentence of teaching that works: "*Stop thinking about where your feet go. Feel the edge. Let the body answer.*" Yuji is earnest, annoyingly so, and keeps saying thank you. "*Stop thanking me. I wanted you dead six weeks ago.*" He doesn't say it. He teaches the kid properly, and lets that be the answer. Panda watches from the edge of the yard and, at the end of the week, sits on the steps beside him again.
- *Closing image:* Panda and Kusakabe on the steps, not talking, watching Yuji train alone in the dusk.

**Ch 24. December Twenty-Third** [4,200]
- *Beats:* the night before Shinjuku. He can't sleep. Sofa, dark, the water stain. Two years he's meant to report it. A quiet laugh. Tomorrow Gojo fights the King of Curses, and if Gojo loses, everyone left goes in, one after another. He thinks about running: a bag, his sister, a small town in the mountains. He thinks about it for a long time. Then Panda holding up the rock. Miwa alive behind him. Yuji, sixteen, earnest, carrying a monster and still saying thank you. Yaga, and the last of the debt. "*The kids are going to fight tomorrow. And I'm the adult.*" Not courage. A fact, like a stone. He takes the sword from the wall and checks the edge because his hands need something to do. Dawn, grey and cold. "*Probably going to die. Probably. Not definitely.*" *Shikata ga nai.* He draws the circle around himself, just to feel it.
- *Closing image:* he walks out the door. **End the chapter there. Do not show anything of the battle.**

**Ch 25. Sunday (Epilogue)** [2,000]
- *Canon anchors:* he lived, with two long scars across his chest. He's now head of the New Shadow Style, its old binding vows lifted.
- *Beats:* a broken sword in a cupboard he doesn't open. "*Promotion. The one thing I spent my whole life avoiding.*" Sunday at his sister's. Lavender. Tea too weak. Today she knows him straight away. "You look tired." "Yeah. Long few months." "Were you working?" A pause with everything in it: Shibuya, the rock, Panda's paw, Miwa, the ice, Yaga, Yuji, Shinjuku. "Yeah. Bit of work." She nods and turns back to the window. Salarymen, students, a woman with a dog. Nobody looking up. He doesn't need them to.
- *Closing lines (keep this meaning):* his circle is still small: his sister's flat, some students, a school of swordsmanship, a cup of tea. But for the first time in a long time it doesn't feel like hiding. It feels like enough.

## 6. TTS rules (for every `chNN_tts.txt`)

The audio is one narrator reading everything, so the text must make sense by ear alone.

- **Plain text only.** No markdown, asterisks, underscores, hashes, brackets, bullet points or emoji. Nothing the voice might read aloud as a symbol.
- **Start each file** with: "Chapter [number, written as a word]. [Title]." Then a blank line.
- **Inner thoughts:** italics disappear in audio, so make each thought clear by context. Keep it short and put it in its own paragraph. Add "he thinks" only when it could otherwise be mistaken for narration, and don't overdo it. Example: *Another day of pretending I'm not tired* becomes "Another day of pretending I'm not tired, he thinks."
- **Scene breaks:** replace `---` with a blank line, then start the next scene with a short sentence that re-anchors time or place ("Later that night." "Sunday.").
- **Numbers:** write as words where they'd be spoken ("six ten," "B five F," "twenty-fourth of December").
- **Japanese words:** use sparingly, and always clear from context. "Shikata ga nai" should be followed once in the book by its meaning, "it can't be helped."
- **Avoid tongue-twisters** and very long sentences. If a sentence is hard to read aloud, split it.
- **Name spellings to keep consistent:** Kusakabe, Atsuya, Panda, Maki, Inumaki, Toge, Gojo, Yaga, Nanami, Miwa, Kenjaku, Uraume, Yuki Tsukumo, Ijichi, Yuji Itadori, Yuta, Gakuganji, Takeru, Sukuna, Jogo, Choso, Shibuya, Shinjuku. Pronunciation hints (for reference only, do not write them into the text): Kusakabe (koo-sah-kah-beh), Uraume (oo-rah-oo-meh), Kenjaku (ken-jah-koo), Inumaki (ee-noo-mah-kee), shikata ga nai (shee-kah-tah gah nai).

**The Circle That Doesn't Need a Name**
*A Kusakabe story*

### I. The Grade 1 Who Didn't Want the Job

The alarm goes off at 6:10.

Kusakabe Atsuya lies there and looks at the ceiling of a flat that is neither nice nor bad. A water mark in the corner he's been meaning to report for two years. He won't report it this year either.

*Grade 1 sorcerer,* he thinks. *One of the strongest in the country, apparently. Can't be bothered to email the landlord.*

He gets up because the body decides to. It's the only reliable thing about him.

Coffee, instant. Not good. He drinks it standing at the window, watching salarymen cross the street below, briefcases swinging, ties already loosened. Ordinary people with ordinary problems. Mortgages. Bosses. Trains. He envies them in a vague, tired way. Nobody ever asked a salaryman to walk into a building full of curses before breakfast.

He has no innate technique. In the jujutsu world, that's like being born without a surname. The clans don't look at you. The higher-ups don't look at you. You don't get handed anything. What he has, he built: the New Shadow Style, years of drawing the same small circle around himself until his body could do it in his sleep. Simple Domain. The reach of one arm and one sword. Anything that crosses the line, he feels, and his hands answer before his head does.

It's not glamorous. It's not Limitless. It's a craft, like a carpenter's, or a barista's. You practise until you can't get it wrong.

He rinses the cup and puts it upside down on the rack.

*Another day of pretending I'm not tired.*

---

At Jujutsu High, the second-years are waiting for him. Or rather, they're not waiting for him. They're doing whatever they want, and he's late.

Maki is already in the training yard, swinging a staff with the kind of intensity he finds exhausting just to watch. Panda is lying on the grass like an actual panda in an actual zoo. Inumaki is sitting on the steps, scarf up to his nose, saying "Salmon" to nobody.

"You're late," Maki says, without stopping.

He doesn't explain. He never explains. "Yeah."

"Why?"

"Traffic."

He walked. There's no traffic between his flat and the school. She knows it, he knows she knows it, and she lets it go. That's the arrangement. He doesn't explain himself, and they don't push. It works because none of them cares enough to fight about it.

He teaches the way he does everything: as little as necessary, as precisely as possible. He watches Maki's footwork and tells her one thing: "Your back heel lifts before you strike. They'll see it coming." She grunts. Next time, the heel stays down. That's a good day's teaching. One sentence that works.

Panda rolls over on the grass. "Sensei. You ever think about quitting?"

"Every day."

"Why don't you?"

He thinks about it honestly, which surprises him.

"Haven't found anything else that pays like this and lets me complain this much."

Panda laughs. Kusakabe doesn't. It wasn't entirely a joke.

### II. The Debt

On Sundays, he visits his sister.

It's the one appointment he never misses, never complains about, and never tells anyone at school about. Not because it's a secret, exactly. Because it's *his.* Some things you keep inside the circle.

His sister's flat is quiet, warm, and smells of the cheap lavender she likes. She sits by the window in her chair. Some days she remembers who he is immediately. Some days it takes a minute. Some days she calls him by their father's name, and he answers to it.

Years ago, her son Takeru died in an accident. Small, sudden, the kind of thing that makes no sense no matter how many times you go over it. She broke. Not in a dramatic way. In the quiet, permanent way. Memory gone in patches. Body that wouldn't do what she asked.

Kusakabe took her on. Nobody asked him to. Nobody would have blamed him if he hadn't. He just did. Paid for the flat. Arranged the carers. Came on Sundays. Sat with her. Made tea. Listened to the same stories over and over, the parts she could still reach.

*It's not kindness,* he tells himself. *It's just what you do. She's my sister.*

He doesn't think too hard about it. Thinking too hard about it would mean feeling it, and he's not built for that much feeling. So he does the tasks instead. Tea. Blinds. Medicine. Small circle. The tasks are easier than the feelings, and they get the same thing done.

And then there's the other thing. The thing nobody knows.

When it was at its worst, when she wouldn't eat and barely spoke, Masamichi Yaga came to him. The principal. The man who made Panda. A craftsman of the forbidden, the kind of knowledge that could get you executed. Yaga looked at Kusakabe's sister and Kusakabe's grief and offered something no one else in the world could: Takeru's soul, given form again, as an independent cursed corpse. Not the boy back, exactly. But the boy, in a way. A child who could sit on his mother's lap.

Kusakabe said yes. Of course he said yes.

He's never forgotten that. He never will. It's a debt so big there's no way to pay it back, so he doesn't try to. He just carries it. A weight in his pocket that he checks, sometimes, like a coin.

*I owe him everything,* he thinks, on the train home. *And I don't even like him that much.*

That's how debts work. You don't have to like the person. You just have to remember.

### VI. Under the Rock (excerpt)

He doesn't fully understand what happens next. Nobody does, at the time.

What he knows: the earth shakes. The air turns into something solid. Fire, and then something worse than fire. A pressure that flattens everything. The building groans like an animal. Concrete splits. The world comes down.

When he opens his eyes, it's dark.

He's on his back. There's a slab of concrete on top of him, a big one. Not crushing him. Somehow it caught at an angle on something else, leaving a pocket of space just big enough for a man to lie in. Dust everywhere. In his mouth, in his eyes. The smell of burnt stone.

His right hand is still gripping his katana.

He doesn't remember choosing to hold onto it. The body did that. The body always does that.

He lies there.

He could try to move. He could test the slab. He could call out. He doesn't do any of it.

He just lies there in the dark, holding the sword, breathing dust.

*So this is it,* he thinks. Not dramatically. Quietly. *Grade 1. No technique. Lied to a student to avoid a basement. Stood still while the King of Curses walked past. Got buried under a building I didn't even get to fight in.*

*What was any of it for?*

He thinks about his sister by her window. The lavender. The tea.

He thinks about Takeru on her lap.

He thinks about Yaga, and the debt in his pocket, still unpaid.

He thinks about his flat, the water stain on the ceiling he never reported. The sofa. The days that disappeared into nothing because he didn't want to fill them with anything that might demand something from him.

*I avoided everything,* he thinks. *And it still found me.*

That's the thing about avoidance. It feels like control. It feels like keeping yourself safe. But the world doesn't care what you avoided. The world comes down on you anyway, and you end up exactly here: on your back, in the dark, under a rock, holding a sword you never got to use, wondering what you saved yourself *for.*

He's not afraid, exactly. Fear needs energy. He doesn't have any.

He's just lost.

Minutes pass. Maybe longer. Distant sounds. Explosions. Screaming. Then quiet.

He closes his eyes.

*Maybe if I lie here long enough, it'll all be over and someone else will have dealt with it.*

---

Then the slab moves.

A grinding sound. Light, sudden and harsh. Dust pouring down. The concrete shifts, tilts and *lifts*, and he squints up into the grey Shibuya sky.

Panda is standing over him, holding the slab over his head with both arms, fur matted with dust and blood.

"Sensei," Panda says. "You alive?"

For a moment, Kusakabe can't answer.

The student he lied to, to avoid going down. The cursed corpse made by the same man who gave his sister back her son. Panda, who is like Takeru in the way that matters. Panda, who didn't have to come looking.

"Yeah," Kusakabe says. His voice is rough with dust. "Yeah. I'm alive."

Panda tosses the slab aside like it's a sofa cushion and holds out a paw.

Kusakabe looks at it.

Then he takes it, and lets himself be pulled up.

Standing in the ruins, sword still in hand, he looks at Panda and almost says something. *Sorry about B5F.* Or *thank you.* Or *I didn't deserve that.*

He doesn't say any of it. That's not how he works.

He just says, "Let's go."

And Panda, who maybe understands more than he lets on, says, "Okay, sensei."

### X. The Morning Before (excerpt)

The night before Shinjuku, he can't sleep.

He lies on his sofa in the dark, staring at the water stain on the ceiling. Two years he's been meaning to report it. He laughs, once, quietly, at nothing.

He thinks about running. He really does. Pack a bag. Take his sister somewhere quiet, a small town, the mountains. Nobody would come looking. A Grade 1 with no technique: who'd miss him?

He thinks about it for a long time.

Then he thinks about Panda holding up the rock.

He thinks about Miwa, behind him, alive.

He thinks about Yuji, earnest, idiotic, sixteen, carrying a monster in his chest and still saying thank you.

He thinks: *the kids are going to fight tomorrow. With their lives. They're children.*

*And I'm the adult.*

That's all. It isn't courage. He'd laugh at anyone who called it that. It's just a fact, sitting in his chest like a stone.

He takes his sword down from the wall. Checks the edge. Not because it needs checking. Because his hands need something to do.

*Probably going to die,* he thinks, almost conversationally. *Probably. Not definitely.*

*Shikata ga nai.*

He draws the circle around himself, just to feel it. The reach of one arm and one sword. Small. Plain. Everything he has.

He walks out the door.
